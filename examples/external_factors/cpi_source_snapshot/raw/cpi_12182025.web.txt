L181:     CONSUMER PRICE INDEX - NOVEMBER 2025
L182:     The Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) increased 0.2 percent on a seasonally adjusted basis over the
L183:     2 months from September 2025 to November 2025, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported today. Over the last 12
L184:     months, the all items index increased 2.7 percent before seasonal adjustment. BLS did not collect survey data for
L185:     October 2025 due to a lapse in appropriations.
L186:     The seasonally adjusted index for all items less food and energy rose 0.2 percent over the 2 months ending in November.
L187:     From September to November, the index for shelter increased 0.2 percent. The energy index rose 1.1 percent over the
L188:     same 2-month period and the food index increased 0.1 percent. Other indexes which increased over the 2 months ending
L189:     in November include household furnishings and operations, communication, and personal care. In contrast, the indexes
L190:     for lodging away from home, recreation, and apparel decreased over the same 2-month period.
L191: 
L192:     The all items index rose 2.7 percent for the 12 months ending November, after rising 3.0 percent over the 12 months
L193:     ending September. The all items less food and energy index rose 2.6 percent over the last 12 months. The energy index
L194:     increased 4.2 percent for the 12 months ending November. The food index increased 2.6 percent over the last year.
L195:     --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
L196:     		  		     Federal Government Shutdown
L197: 
L198:     BLS did not collect survey data for October 2025 due to a lapse in appropriations. BLS was unable to
L199:     retroactively collect these data. For a few indexes, BLS uses nonsurvey data sources instead of survey data
L200:     to make the index calculations. BLS was able to retroactively acquire most of the nonsurvey data for October.
L201:     CPI data collection resumed on November 14, 2025.
L202: 
L203:     Additional information about the impact of the shutdown on CPI data is available online at

L247:     Food
L248: 
L249:     The index for food increased 2.6 percent over the last year. The index for food at home rose 1.9 percent over the 12
L250:     months ending in November. The meats, poultry, fish, and eggs index rose 4.7 percent over the last 12 months. The
L251:     index for nonalcoholic beverages increased 4.3 percent over the same period and the index for other food at home rose
L252:     1.3 percent. The cereals and bakery products index increased 1.9 percent over the 12 months ending in November and the
L253:     fruits and vegetables index rose 0.1 percent over the year. In contrast, the index for dairy and related products
L254:     decreased 1.6 percent over the same period.
L255: 
L256:     The food away from home index rose 3.7 percent over the last year. The index for full service meals rose 4.3 percent
L257:     and the index for limited service meals rose 3.0 percent over the same period.
L258:     Energy
L259: 
L260:     The index for energy increased 4.2 percent over the past 12 months. The gasoline index rose 0.9 percent over this
L261:     12-month span and the fuel oil index increased 11.3 percent over the same period. The index for electricity increased
L262:     6.9 percent over the last 12 months and the index for natural gas rose 9.1 percent.
L263: 
L264: 
L265:     All items less food and energy
L266:     The index for all items less food and energy rose 2.6 percent over the past 12 months. The shelter index increased
L267:     3.0 percent over the last year. Other indexes with notable increases over the last year include medical care (+2.9
L268:     percent), household furnishings and operations (+4.6 percent), recreation (+1.8 percent), and used cars and trucks
L269:     (+3.6 percent).
L270: 
L271:     Not seasonally adjusted CPI measures
L272:     The Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) increased 2.7 percent over the last 12 months to an index
L273:     level of 324.122 (1982-84=100).
L274: 
L275:     The Consumer Price Index for Urban Wage Earners and Clerical Workers (CPI-W) increased 2.7 percent over the last 12
L276:     months to an index level of 317.414 (1982-84=100).
L277: 
L278:     The Chained Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (C-CPI-U) increased 2.6 percent over the last 12 months.
