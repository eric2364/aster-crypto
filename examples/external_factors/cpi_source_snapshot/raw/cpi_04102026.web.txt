L181:     CONSUMER PRICE INDEX - MARCH 2026
L182:     The Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) increased 0.9 percent on a seasonally adjusted basis in March,
L183:     after rising 0.3 percent in February, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported today. Over the last 12 months, the
L184:     all items index increased 3.3 percent before seasonal adjustment.
L185: 
L186:     The index for energy rose 10.9 percent in March, led by a 21.2-percent increase in the index for gasoline which
L187:     accounted for nearly three quarters of the monthly all items increase. The shelter index also increased in March,
L188:     rising 0.3 percent. The index for food was unchanged over the month as the index for food away from home rose 0.2
L189:     percent, while the index for food at home fell 0.2 percent.
L190: 
L191:     The index for all items less food and energy rose 0.2 percent in March. Indexes that increased over the month include
L192:     airline fares, apparel, household furnishings and operations, education, and new vehicles. Conversely, the indexes for
L193:     medical care, personal care, and used cars and trucks were among the major indexes that decreased in March.
L194: 
L195:     The all items index rose 3.3 percent for the 12 months ending March, after rising 2.4 percent for the 12 months ending
L196:     February. The all items less food and energy index rose 2.6 percent over the year, following a 2.5-percent increase over
L197:     the 12 months ending February. The energy index increased 12.5 percent for the 12 months ending March. The food index
L198:     increased 2.7 percent over the last year.

L240:     Food
L241: 
L242:     The index for food was unchanged in March after rising 0.4 percent in February. The food at home index declined 0.2
L243:     percent over the month. Four of the six major grocery store food group indexes decreased in March. The index for meats,
L244:     poultry, fish, and eggs decreased 0.6 percent over the month as the index for eggs declined 3.4 percent. The cereals and
L245:     bakery products index also decreased 0.6 percent in March, as did the dairy and related products index. The index for
L246:     nonalcoholic beverages fell 0.3 percent over the month.
L247: 
L248:     In contrast, the fruits and vegetables index rose 1.0 percent in March. The index for other food at home was unchanged
L249:     over the month.
L250: 
L251:     The food away from home index rose 0.2 percent in March. The index for full service meals rose 0.3 percent over the
L252:     month and the index for limited service meals rose 0.2 percent.
L253: 
L254:     The food at home index rose 1.9 percent over the 12 months ending in March. The index for other food at home rose 2.9
L255:     percent over the last 12 months. The fruits and vegetables index increased 4.0 percent over the same period and the
L256:     nonalcoholic beverages index rose 4.7 percent. The index for cereals and bakery products increased 2.1 percent over the
L257:     12 months ending in March. In contrast, the dairy and related products index fell 1.6 percent over the year and the
L258:     meats, poultry, fish, and eggs index decreased 0.9 percent over the same period.
L259: 
L260:     The food away from home index rose 3.8 percent over the last year. The index for full service meals rose 4.3 percent and
L261:     the index for limited service meals rose 3.2 percent over the same period.
L262: 
L263:     Energy
L264:     The index for energy increased 10.9 percent in March, the largest monthly increase in the index since September 2005.
L265:     The gasoline index increased 21.2 percent over the month, the largest monthly increase since the series was first
L266:     published in 1967. (Before seasonal adjustment, gasoline prices increased 24.9 percent in March.) The index for
L267:     electricity rose 0.8 percent in March. The fuel oil index increased 30.7 percent over the month, the largest monthly
L268:     increase in the index since February 2000. Conversely, the index for natural gas decreased 0.9 percent over the month.
L269: 
L270:     The index for energy increased 12.5 percent over the past 12 months and the index for gasoline rose 18.9 percent. The
L271:     electricity index increased 4.6 percent over the last 12 months and the natural gas index rose 6.4 percent.
L272: 
L273:     All items less food and energy
L274:     The index for all items less food and energy rose 0.2 percent in March, as it did in February. The shelter index
L275:     increased 0.3 percent over the month as did the owners' equivalent rent index. The index for rent increased 0.2 percent
L276:     in March. The lodging away from home index rose 0.2 percent over the month.
