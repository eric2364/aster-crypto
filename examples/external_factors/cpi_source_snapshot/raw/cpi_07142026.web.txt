L181:     CONSUMER PRICE INDEX - JUNE 2026
L182:     The Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) decreased 0.4 percent on a seasonally adjusted basis in June
L183:     after rising 0.5 percent in May, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported today. This decline in the all items
L184:     index was the largest 1-month decrease since April 2020 when it fell 0.8 percent. Over the last 12 months, the all
L185:     items index increased 3.5 percent before seasonal adjustment.
L186:     The index for energy fell 5.7 percent in June after rising 3.9 percent in May, 3.8 percent in April, and 10.9 percent
L187:     in March. The energy index was the largest contributor to the monthly all items decrease, more than offsetting
L188:     increases in other indexes including those for shelter and food. The index for food increased 0.2 percent over the
L189:     month, as did the index for food at home and the index for food away from home.
L190:     The index for all items less food and energy was unchanged in June. Indexes that decreased over the month include
L191:     motor vehicle insurance, communication, apparel, medical care, and used cars and trucks. Conversely, the indexes for
L192:     recreation, household furnishings and operations, and personal care were among the major indexes that increased in
L193:     June.
L194: 
L195:     The all items index rose 3.5 percent for the 12 months ending June after rising 4.2 percent for the 12 months ending
L196:     May. The all items less food and energy index rose 2.6 percent over the year, following a 2.9-percent increase over
L197:     the 12 months ending May. The energy index increased 15.7 percent for the 12 months ending June. The food index
L198:     increased 3.0 percent over the last year.

L239:     Food
L240: 
L241:     The food index rose 0.2 percent in June, as it did in May. The index for food at home also increased 0.2 percent over
L242:     the month. Four of the six major grocery store food group indexes increased in June. The meats, poultry, fish, and
L243:     eggs index increased 0.6 percent over the month as the eggs index rose 4.3 percent. The index for other food at home
L244:     increased 0.5 percent in June, and the index for dairy and related products rose 1.2 percent. The cereals and bakery
L245:     products index increased 0.3 percent over the month.
L246: 
L247:     In contrast, the index for nonalcoholic beverages fell 1.5 percent in June as the index for coffee declined 2.0 percent.
L248:     The fruits and vegetables index decreased 0.2 percent over the month.
L249: 
L250:     The food away from home index rose 0.2 percent in June. The index for full service meals rose 0.4 percent, and the
L251:     index for limited service meals rose 0.1 percent over the month.
L252: 
L253:     The index for food at home rose 2.7 percent over the 12 months ending in June. The fruits and vegetables index rose 5.3
L254:     percent over the last 12 months. The index for other food at home increased 2.4 percent, and the index for meats,
L255:     poultry, fish, and eggs rose 2.6 percent over the year. The nonalcoholic beverages index increased 2.9 percent over the
L256:     12 months ending in June, and the cereals and bakery products index rose 2.4 percent over the same period. The index
L257:     for dairy and related products rose 0.4 percent over the year.
L258: 
L259:     The food away from home index rose 3.4 percent over the last year. The index for full service meals rose 3.7 percent,
L260:     and the index for limited service meals rose 3.1 percent over the 12 months ending in June.
L261: 
L262:     Energy
L263:     The index for energy decreased 5.7 percent in June, the largest 1-month decline since April 2020. The gasoline index
L264:     decreased 9.7 percent over the month. (Before seasonal adjustment, gasoline prices also decreased 9.7 percent in June.)
L265:     The index for electricity fell 1.0 percent in June. Conversely, the index for natural gas increased 0.5 percent over
L266:     the same period.
L267:     The index for energy increased 15.7 percent over the past 12 months due in large part to the index for gasoline rising
L268:     26.7 percent over the same period. The electricity index increased 4.0 percent over the 12 months ending in June, and
L269:     the natural gas index rose 3.0 percent.
