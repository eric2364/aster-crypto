L180:     CONSUMER PRICE INDEX - SEPTEMBER 2025
L181: 
L182:     The Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) increased 0.3 percent on a seasonally adjusted basis in
L183:     September, after rising 0.4 percent in August, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported today. Over the last 12
L184:     months, the all items index increased 3.0 percent before seasonal adjustment. Note that September CPI data collection
L185:     was completed before the lapse in appropriations.
L186: 
L187:     The index for gasoline rose 4.1 percent in September and was the largest factor in the all items monthly increase, as
L188:     the index for energy rose 1.5 percent over the month. The food index increased 0.2 percent over the month as the food
L189:     at home index rose 0.3 percent and the food away from home index increased 0.1 percent.
L190: 
L191:     The index for all items less food and energy rose 0.2 percent in September, after rising 0.3 percent in each of the 2
L192:     preceding months. Indexes that increased over the month include shelter, airline fares, recreation, household
L193:     furnishings and operations, and apparel. The indexes for motor vehicle insurance, used cars and trucks, and
L194:     communication were among the few major indexes that decreased in September.
L195: 
L196:     The all items index rose 3.0 percent for the 12 months ending September, after rising 2.9 percent over the 12 months
L197:     ending August. The all items less food and energy index also rose 3.0 percent over the last 12 months. The energy index
L198:     increased 2.8 percent for the 12 months ending September. The food index increased 3.1 percent over the last year.

L239:     Food
L240: 
L241:     The index for food rose 0.2 percent in September, after rising 0.5 percent in August. The food at home index increased
L242:     0.3 percent over the month. Four of the six major grocery store food group indexes increased in September. The index for
L243:     other food at home rose 0.5 percent over the month after rising 0.1 percent in August. The cereals and bakery products
L244:     index and the nonalcoholic beverages index both increased 0.7 percent in September. The index for meats, poultry, fish,
L245:     and eggs rose 0.3 percent over the month following a 1.0-percent increase in August.
L246: 
L247:     The dairy and related products index declined 0.5 percent in September as the cheese and related products index
L248:     decreased 0.7 percent. The index for fruits and vegetables was unchanged over the month.
L249:     The food away from home index rose 0.1 percent in September. The index for limited service meals rose 0.2 percent over
L250:     the month while the index for full service meals was unchanged.
L251: 
L252:     The index for food at home rose 2.7 percent over the 12 months ending in September, as it did over the 12 months ending
L253:     in August. The meats, poultry, fish, and eggs index rose 5.2 percent over the last 12 months. The index for nonalcoholic
L254:     beverages increased 5.3 percent over the same period and the index for other food at home rose 1.9 percent. The cereals
L255:     and bakery products index increased 1.6 percent over the 12 months ending in September. The index for fruits and
L256:     vegetables rose 1.3 percent and the index for dairy and related products increased 0.7 percent over the same period.
L257: 
L258:     The food away from home index rose 3.7 percent over the last year. The index for full service meals rose 4.2 percent and
L259:     the index for limited service meals rose 3.2 percent over the same period.
L260: 
L261:     Energy
L262: 
L263:     The index for energy increased 1.5 percent in September, after rising 0.7 percent in August. The gasoline index
L264:     increased 4.1 percent over the month. (Before seasonal adjustment, gasoline prices increased 1.1 percent in September.)
L265:     The index for electricity decreased 0.5 percent over the month and the index for natural gas decreased 1.2 percent over
L266:     the same period.
L267:     The index for energy increased 2.8 percent over the past 12 months. The electricity index increased 5.1 percent over the
L268:     12 months ending in September and the natural gas index rose 11.7 percent. In contrast, the index for gasoline fell 0.5
L269:     percent over this 12-month span.
L270: 
L271:     All items less food and energy
L272: 
L273:     The index for all items less food and energy rose 0.2 percent in September, after rising 0.3 percent in each of the
L274:     preceding 2 months. The shelter index increased 0.2 percent over the month. The index for owners' equivalent rent rose
L275:     0.1 percent in September, the smallest 1-month increase in that index since January 2021. The rent index increased 0.2
L276:     percent over the month. The index for lodging away from home rose 1.3 percent in September.
