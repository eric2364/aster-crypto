L181:     CONSUMER PRICE INDEX - DECEMBER 2025
L182:     The Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) increased 0.3 percent on a seasonally adjusted basis in
L183:     December, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported today. Over the last 12 months, the all items index increased
L184:     2.7 percent before seasonal adjustment.
L185: 
L186:     The index for shelter rose 0.4 percent in December and was the largest factor in the all items monthly increase. The
L187:     food index increased 0.7 percent over the month as did the food at home index and the food away from home index. The
L188:     index for energy rose 0.3 percent in December.
L189: 
L190:     The index for all items less food and energy rose 0.2 percent in December. Indexes that increased over the month
L191:     include recreation, airline fares, medical care, apparel, personal care, and education. The indexes for communication,
L192:     used cars and trucks, and household furnishings and operations were among the major indexes that decreased in December.
L193: 
L194:     The all items index rose 2.7 percent for the 12 months ending December, the same increase as over the 12 months ending
L195:     November. The all items less food and energy index rose 2.6 percent over the last 12 months. The energy index increased
L196:     2.3 percent for the 12 months ending December. The food index increased 3.1 percent over the last year.

L239:     Food
L240: 
L241:     The index for food rose 0.7 percent in December as did the index for food at home. Five of the six major grocery store
L242:     food group indexes increased in December. The index for other food at home rose 1.6 percent over the month. The cereals
L243:     and bakery products index increased 0.6 percent in December. The index for fruits and vegetables increased 0.5 percent
L244:     and the index for nonalcoholic beverages increased 0.4 percent. The dairy and related products index rose 0.9 percent
L245:     in December. In contrast, the index for meats, poultry, fish, and eggs decreased 0.2 percent in December, as the index
L246:     for eggs fell 8.2 percent.
L247: 
L248:     The food away from home index also rose 0.7 percent in December. The index for full service meals rose 0.8 percent over
L249:     the month and the index for limited service meals increased 0.6 percent.
L250:     The index for food at home rose 2.4 percent over the 12 months ending in December. The meats, poultry, fish, and eggs
L251:     index rose 3.9 percent over the last 12 months. The index for other food at home increased 2.7 percent over the same
L252:     period and the index for nonalcoholic beverages rose 5.1 percent. The cereals and bakery products index increased 1.5
L253:     percent over the 12 months ending in December. The index for fruits and vegetables rose 0.5 percent over the year. In
L254:     contrast, the dairy and related products index decreased 0.9 percent over the same period.
L255: 
L256:     The food away from home index rose 4.1 percent over the last year. The index for full service meals rose 4.9 percent
L257:     and the index for limited service meals rose 3.3 percent over the same period.
L258: 
L259:     Energy
L260: 
L261:     The index for energy increased 0.3 percent in December. The natural gas index increased 4.4 percent over the same
L262:     period. The index for gasoline decreased 0.5 percent over the month. (Before seasonal adjustment, gasoline prices
L263:     decreased 5.3 percent in December.) The electricity index declined 0.1 percent in December.
L264: 
L265:     The index for energy increased 2.3 percent over the past 12 months. The electricity index increased 6.7 percent over
L266:     the last 12 months and the natural gas index rose 10.8 percent. In contrast, the index for gasoline fell 3.4 percent
L267:     over this 12-month span.
L268:     All items less food and energy
L269: 
L270:     The index for all items less food and energy rose 0.2 percent in December. The shelter index increased 0.4 percent over
L271:     the month. The index for owners' equivalent rent rose 0.3 percent in December as did the index for rent. The lodging
L272:     away from home index rose 2.9 percent over the month.
L273: 
L274:     The index for recreation increased 1.2 percent over the month, the largest 1-month increase ever reported for that
L275:     index, which was first published in 1993. The airline fares index rose 5.2 percent in December and the apparel index
L276:     rose 0.6 percent. The index for personal care rose 0.4 percent over the month and the index for education increased 0.2
L277:     percent.
