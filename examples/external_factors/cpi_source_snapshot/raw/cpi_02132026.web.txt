L181:     CONSUMER PRICE INDEX - JANUARY 2026
L182:     The Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) increased 0.2 percent on a seasonally adjusted basis in
L183:     January, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported today. Over the last 12 months, the all items index increased 2.4
L184:     percent before seasonal adjustment.
L185: 
L186:     The index for shelter rose 0.2 percent in January and was the largest factor in the all items monthly increase. The
L187:     food index increased 0.2 percent over the month as did the food at home index, while the food away from home index rose
L188:     0.1 percent. These increases were partially offset by the index for energy, which fell 1.5 percent in January.
L189: 
L190:     The index for all items less food and energy rose 0.3 percent in January. Indexes that increased over the month include
L191:     airline fares, personal care, recreation, medical care, and communication. The indexes for used cars and trucks,
L192:     household furnishings and operations, and motor vehicle insurance were among the major indexes that decreased in
L193:     January.
L194: 
L195:     The all items index rose 2.4 percent for the 12 months ending January, after rising 2.7 percent for the 12 months
L196:     ending December. The all items less food and energy index rose 2.5 percent over the last 12 months. The energy index
L197:     decreased 0.1 percent for the 12 months ending January. The food index increased 2.9 percent over the last year.

L239:     Food
L240: 
L241:     The index for food rose 0.2 percent in January as did the index for food at home. Five of the six major grocery store
L242:     food group indexes increased in January. The index for cereals and bakery products rose 1.2 percent over the month. The
L243:     meats, poultry, fish, and eggs index increased 0.2 percent in January. The index for nonalcoholic beverages and the
L244:     index for fruits and vegetables both increased 0.1 percent over the month. The dairy and related products index rose
L245:     0.8 percent in January. In contrast, the index for other food at home decreased 0.3 percent in January.
L246: 
L247:     The food away from home index rose 0.1 percent in January. The index for limited service meals increased 0.3 percent,
L248:     while the index for full service meals was unchanged over the month.
L249:     The food at home index rose 2.1 percent over the 12 months ending in January. The index for other food at home rose 2.1
L250:     percent over the last 12 months. The nonalcoholic beverages index increased 4.5 percent over the same period and the
L251:     meats, poultry, fish, and eggs index rose 2.2 percent. The index for cereals and bakery products increased 3.1 percent
L252:     over the 12 months ending in January. The fruits and vegetables index rose 0.8 percent over the year. In contrast, the
L253:     index for dairy and related products decreased 0.3 percent over the same period.
L254: 
L255:     The food away from home index rose 4.0 percent over the last year. The index for full service meals rose 4.7 percent
L256:     and the index for limited service meals rose 3.2 percent over the same period.
L257: 
L258:     Energy
L259: 
L260:     The index for energy decreased 1.5 percent in January. The gasoline index decreased 3.2 percent over the month. (Before
L261:     seasonal adjustment, gasoline prices decreased 2.5 percent in January.) The index for electricity declined 0.1 percent
L262:     in January. In contrast, the natural gas index increased 1.0 percent over the same period.
L263: 
L264:     The index for energy decreased 0.1 percent over the past 12 months. The gasoline index fell 7.5 percent over this
L265:     12-month span. In contrast, the index for electricity increased 6.3 percent over the last 12 months and the index for
L266:     natural gas rose 9.8 percent.
L267:     All items less food and energy
L268: 
L269:     The index for all items less food and energy rose 0.3 percent in January. The shelter index increased 0.2 percent over
L270:     the month. The index for owners' equivalent rent also rose 0.2 percent in January as did the index for rent. The
L271:     lodging away from home index fell 0.1 percent over the month.
L272: 
L273:     The index for airline fares increased 6.5 percent over the month. The personal care index rose 1.2 percent in January
L274:     and the recreation index rose 0.5 percent. The index for communication rose 0.5 percent over the month and the index
L275:     for apparel increased 0.3 percent. The new vehicles index rose 0.1 percent in January.
L276: 
L277:     The medical care index increased 0.3 percent in January. The index for hospital services increased 0.9 percent over the
L278:     month and the index for physicians' services rose 0.3 percent. The prescription drugs index was unchanged in January.
