L181:     CONSUMER PRICE INDEX - AUGUST 2026
L182:     The Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) increased 0.4 percent on a seasonally adjusted basis in August
L183:     after rising 0.1 percent in July, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported today. Over the last 12 months, the all
L184:     items index increased 3.4 percent before seasonal adjustment.
L185: 
L186:     The index for gasoline rose 3.9 percent in August, accounting for over one third of the monthly all items increase. The
L187:     index for energy increased 2.1 percent over the month. The shelter index rose 0.3 percent in August after rising 0.1
L188:     percent in July. The index for food increased 0.1 percent over the month, as the index for food away from home increased
L189:     0.3 percent.
L190: 
L191:     The index for all items less food and energy rose 0.3 percent after increasing 0.2 percent in July. Indexes that
L192:     increased over the month include communication, lodging away from home, airline fares, education, and used cars and
L193:     trucks. Conversely, the index for medical care and the index for motor vehicle insurance were among the major indexes
L194:     that decreased in August.
L195: 
L196:     The all items index rose 3.4 percent for the 12 months ending August as it did for the 12 months ending July. The all
L197:     items less food and energy index rose 2.4 percent over the year, following a 2.5-percent increase over the 12 months
L198:     ending July. The energy index increased 16.3 percent for the 12 months ending August. The food index increased 2.7
L199:     percent over the last year.

L240:     Food
L241: 
L242:     The food index rose 0.1 percent in August, as it did in July. The index for food at home was unchanged over the month.
L243:     Four of the six major grocery store food group indexes increased in August. The meats, poultry, fish, and eggs index
L244:     increased 0.1 percent over the month, as the eggs index rose 2.9 percent. The index for other food at home also
L245:     increased 0.1 percent in August. The nonalcoholic beverages index increased 0.2 percent over the month, and the dairy
L246:     and related products index rose 0.3 percent. The index for cereals and bakery products was unchanged in August.
L247: 
L248:     In contrast, the fruits and vegetables index declined 0.4 percent in August after falling in the preceding 2 months. The
L249:     index for lettuce continued to fall, declining 6.2 percent in August after decreasing 16.4 percent in July.
L250:     The food away from home index rose 0.3 percent in August. The index for full service meals rose 0.4 percent, and the
L251:     index for limited service meals was unchanged over the month.
L252: 
L253:     The food at home index rose 2.2 percent over the 12 months ending in August. The index for other food at home increased
L254:     2.4 percent, and the index for fruits and vegetables rose 3.2 percent over the year. The nonalcoholic beverages index
L255:     increased 3.7 percent over the 12 months ending in August, and the cereals and bakery products index rose 2.6 percent
L256:     over the same period. The index for meats, poultry, fish, and eggs increased 1.1 percent over the last 12 months. In
L257:     contrast, the dairy and related products index fell 0.3 percent over the year.
L258: 
L259:     The food away from home index rose 3.4 percent over the last year. The index for full service meals rose 3.5 percent,
L260:     and the index for limited service meals rose 3.2 percent over the 12 months ending in August.
L261: 
L262:     Energy
L263: 
L264:     The index for energy increased 2.1 percent in August after falling 1.5 percent in July. The gasoline index increased 3.9
L265:     percent over the month. (Before seasonal adjustment, gasoline prices increased 2.5 percent in August.) Conversely, the
L266:     index for natural gas decreased 1.1 percent in August, and the index for electricity fell 0.2 percent.
L267:     The index for energy increased 16.3 percent over the past 12 months due in large part to the index for gasoline rising
L268:     27.4 percent over the same period. The natural gas index increased 4.4 percent over the 12 months ending in August, and
L269:     the electricity index rose 3.8 percent.
L270: 
L271:     All items less food and energy
L272: 
L273:     The index for all items less food and energy rose 0.3 percent in August after rising 0.2 percent in July. The shelter
L274:     index increased 0.3 percent over the month after rising 0.1 percent in July. The index for owners' equivalent rent rose
L275:     0.2 percent in August as did the index for rent. The lodging away from home index rose 2.4 percent in August after
L276:     falling 2.8 percent the previous month.
