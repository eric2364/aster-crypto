L181:     CONSUMER PRICE INDEX - MAY 2026
L182:     The Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) increased 0.5 percent on a seasonally adjusted basis in May,
L183:     after rising 0.6 percent in April, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported today. Over the last 12 months, the all
L184:     items index increased 4.2 percent before seasonal adjustment.
L185: 
L186:     The index for energy rose 3.9 percent in May, after rising 3.8 percent in April and 10.9 percent in March. The energy
L187:     index accounted for over sixty percent of the monthly all items increase. The index for shelter also increased in May,
L188:     rising 0.3 percent. The food index increased 0.2 percent over the month as the food at home index rose 0.1 percent and
L189:     the food away from home index increased 0.3 percent.
L190: 
L191:     The index for all items less food and energy rose 0.2 percent in May. Indexes that increased over the month include
L192:     communication, airline fares, medical care, personal care, and recreation. Conversely, the indexes for motor vehicle
L193:     insurance, household furnishings and operations, and new vehicles were among the major indexes that decreased in May.
L194: 
L195:     The all items index rose 4.2 percent for the 12 months ending May, after rising 3.8 percent for the 12 months ending
L196:     April. The all items less food and energy index rose 2.9 percent over the year, following a 2.8-percent increase over
L197:     the 12 months ending April. The energy index increased 23.5 percent for the 12 months ending May. The food index
L198:     increased 3.1 percent over the last year.

L240:     Food
L241: 
L242:     The index for food rose 0.2 percent in May after rising 0.5 percent in April. The food at home index increased 0.1
L243:     percent over the month. Three of the six major grocery store food group indexes increased in May. The index for
L244:     nonalcoholic beverages increased 0.6 percent over the month as the index for beverage materials including coffee and
L245:     tea rose 1.1 percent. The cereals and bakery products index increased 0.4 percent in May and the fruits and vegetables
L246:     index rose 0.2 percent.
L247: 
L248:     In contrast, the index for dairy and related products fell 0.6 percent in May as the index for cheese declined 2.9
L249:     percent. The meats, poultry, fish, and eggs index decreased 0.2 percent over the month. The index for other food at home
L250:     was unchanged in May.
L251:     The food away from home index rose 0.3 percent in May. The index for limited service meals and the index for full
L252:     service meals both also rose 0.3 percent over the month.
L253: 
L254:     The index for food at home rose 2.7 percent over the 12 months ending in May. The fruits and vegetables index rose 6.1
L255:     percent over the last 12 months. The index for nonalcoholic beverages increased 5.8 percent over the same period and the
L256:     index for other food at home rose 2.0 percent. The meats, poultry, fish, and eggs index increased 1.8 percent over the
L257:     12 months ending in May and the cereals and bakery products index rose 1.9 percent over the same period. In contrast,
L258:     the index for dairy and related products fell 1.0 percent over the year.
L259: 
L260:     The food away from home index rose 3.5 percent over the last year. The index for full service meals rose 3.8 percent and
L261:     the index for limited service meals rose 3.3 percent over the same period.
L262: 
L263:     Energy
L264: 
L265:     The index for energy increased 3.9 percent in May, after rising 3.8 percent in April. The gasoline index increased 7.0
L266:     percent over the month. (Before seasonal adjustment, gasoline prices increased 8.6 percent in May.) The index for
L267:     electricity rose 0.6 percent in May. Conversely, the index for natural gas decreased 0.5 percent over the same period.
L268:     The index for energy increased 23.5 percent over the past 12 months and the index for gasoline rose 40.5 percent. The
L269:     electricity index increased 5.9 percent over the 12 months ending in May and the natural gas index rose 3.0 percent.
L270: 
L271:     All items less food and energy
L272: 
L273:     The index for all items less food and energy rose 0.2 percent in May, after rising 0.4 percent in April. The shelter
L274:     index increased 0.3 percent over the month. The index for owners' equivalent rent rose 0.3 percent in May and the index
L275:     for rent increased 0.4 percent. The lodging away from home index also rose 0.4 percent over the month.
L276: 
L277:     The index for communication increased 1.3 percent over the month, after falling 0.2 percent in April. The airline fares
L278:     index rose 2.7 percent in May and the personal care index rose 1.0 percent. The index for recreation rose 0.3 percent
