L181:     CONSUMER PRICE INDEX - FEBRUARY 2026
L182:     The Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) increased 0.3 percent on a seasonally adjusted basis in
L183:     February, after rising 0.2 percent in January, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported today. Over the last 12
L184:     months, the all items index increased 2.4 percent before seasonal adjustment.
L185: 
L186:     The index for shelter rose 0.2 percent in February and was the largest factor in the all items monthly increase. The
L187:     food index increased 0.4 percent over the month as did the food at home index, while the food away from home index rose
L188:     0.3 percent. The index for energy also increased in February, rising 0.6 percent.
L189: 
L190:     The index for all items less food and energy rose 0.2 percent in February. Indexes that increased over the month
L191:     include medical care, apparel, household furnishings and operations, airline fares, and education. Conversely, the
L192:     indexes for communication, used cars and trucks, motor vehicle insurance, and personal care were among the major
L193:     indexes that decreased in February.
L194: 
L195:     The all items index rose 2.4 percent for the 12 months ending February, the same increase as reported for the 12 months
L196:     ending January. The all items less food and energy index rose 2.5 percent over the year, also the same increase as
L197:     reported for the 12 months ending in January. The energy index increased 0.5 percent for the 12 months ending February.
L198:     The food index increased 3.1 percent over the last year.

L240:     Food
L241: 
L242:     The index for food rose 0.4 percent in February as did the index for food at home. Both indexes also increased 0.2
L243:     percent in January. Three of the six major grocery store food group indexes increased in February. The index for other
L244:     food at home rose 0.8 percent in February, as the index for candy and chewing gum rose 3.7 percent. The fruits and
L245:     vegetables index increased 1.4 percent over the month, and the nonalcoholic beverages index rose 0.8 percent.
L246: 
L247:     In contrast, the index for dairy and related products decreased 0.6 percent in February, with the index for cheese
L248:     declining 1.2 percent. The cereals and bakery products index declined 0.2 percent over the month, after increasing 1.2
L249:     percent in January. The index for meats, poultry, fish, and eggs was unchanged in February with its components mixed.
L250:     The food away from home index rose 0.3 percent in February. The index for limited service meals and the index for full
L251:     service meals also each rose 0.3 percent over the month.
L252: 
L253:     The food at home index rose 2.4 percent over the 12 months ending in February. The index for other food at home rose
L254:     3.3 percent over the last 12 months. The nonalcoholic beverages index increased 5.6 percent over the same period and
L255:     the fruits and vegetables index rose 2.7 percent. The index for cereals and bakery products increased 2.7 percent over
L256:     the 12 months ending in February. Despite a 42.1 percent drop in the eggs index, the meats, poultry, fish, and eggs
L257:     index rose 0.4 percent over the year and the dairy and related products index increased 0.1 percent over the same
L258:     period.
L259: 
L260:     The food away from home index rose 3.9 percent over the last year. The index for full service meals rose 4.6 percent
L261:     and the index for limited service meals rose 3.2 percent over the same period.
L262: 
L263:     Energy
L264: 
L265:     The index for energy increased 0.6 percent in February, after falling 1.5 percent in January. The gasoline index
L266:     increased 0.8 percent over the month. (Before seasonal adjustment, gasoline prices increased 3.3 percent in February.)
L267:     The index for natural gas rose 3.1 percent in February. Conversely, the electricity index decreased 0.7 percent over
L268:     the same period.
L269:     The index for energy increased 0.5 percent over the past 12 months. The electricity index increased 4.8 percent over
L270:     the last 12 months and the natural gas index rose 10.9 percent. In contrast, the index for gasoline fell 5.6 percent
L271:     over this span.
L272: 
L273:     All items less food and energy
L274: 
L275:     The index for all items less food and energy rose 0.2 percent in February, following a 0.3-percent increase in January.
L276:     The shelter index increased 0.2 percent over the month as did the owners' equivalent rent index. The index for rent
L277:     increased 0.1 percent in February, the smallest 1-month increase in that index since January 2021. The lodging away
L278:     from home index rose 1.0 percent over the month.
