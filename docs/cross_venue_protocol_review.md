## Defensible hypothesis

Past realized funding yields may persist differently across Binance and Bybit. A slow strategy could hold equal underlying quantities, short the venue with the higher **lagged per-hour realized yield** and long the lower-yield venue. It earns or pays the actual subsequent settlements while bearing cross-venue basis, execution, collateral and counterparty risk. This is not contemporaneous quote arbitrage, a guaranteed funding receipt or a trade on the future final funding rate.

Use one fixed common-underlying universe, one multi-day calendar lookback, one daily review clock, one fixed maximum holding period and one conservative cost gate. Freeze their exact values, direction/exit/cooldown and risk rules before outcomes; no asset ranking, parameter grid or reversal after losses. Inactive allocations stay cash. Different assets and policy variants do not become independent economic edges.

## Causal normalized predictor

Maintain separate settlement streams per venue/native contract. For already observed consecutive settlements, let `delta_k=(settlement_k-settlement_(k-1))/hour` and `h_k=rate_k/delta_k`. Compare duration-weighted yields `sum(rate_k)/sum(delta_k)` over **fully completed intervals** lying inside the prescribed trailing calendar window. Equal-weight averages of per-settlement rates distort comparisons when one venue settles hourly and another every eight hours. Do not use the next, not-yet-observed settlement to determine an earlier interval or normalize every historic rate by today's eight-hour setting.

The first rate inside the permitted year has no usable preceding interval unless that predecessor is also inside the year; omit it and require sufficient warmup. Do not borrow earlier-year history. Use the latest actually received observations only. Required availability is settlement plus a prospectively declared processing/publication assumption, or verified historical receipt plus processing if supplied. All included observations must be available strictly before the decision. Never backfill a later receipt into an earlier forecast. Cash settlement remains at its actual event time, separately from predictor availability.

Freeze minimum calendar coverage, permitted interval changes and freshness/expiry before outcomes. A feature expires when either venue becomes stale; extending the other venue's last value indefinitely is not a live differential. A past-interval-based expiry can be causal but cannot announce an unobserved next schedule. Reversal/exit on stale information must use actual later fillable prices and existing pending-order handling.

**Critical limitation:** a long inter-event gap can represent missing history or a genuine schedule change. History alone cannot always distinguish them. Normalize only corroborated consecutive events; otherwise mark the interval ambiguous and suppress affected signals. The resulting `h` is a realized-cash-per-hour proxy, not independently verified scheduled accrual. Current interval metadata cannot resolve past ambiguity. Binance's funding history includes settlement rate/time and an associated funding mark; its adjustment endpoint is not a dated historical schedule. [Official Binance market-data documentation](https://developers.binance.com/en/docs/catalog/core-trading-derivatives-trading-usd-s-m-futures/api/rest-api/market-data).

Bybit history exposes rate and settlement timestamp, without historical receipt, next-rate announcement or settlement mark. Its documentation permits dynamic settlement-frequency changes. These facts prevent an exact historical information/valuation claim, but do not prohibit a clearly labeled lagged realized-history model. [Funding-history API](https://bybit-exchange.github.io/docs/v5/market/history-fund-rate), [funding mechanism](https://www.bybit.com/en/help-center/article/Funding-fee-calculation).

## Execution and conservative economics

Use two independently pre-funded USDT wallets in one Engine, venue-local **cross margin with cash-only collateral and low gross exposure**, and no borrowing, transfers or pooled-margin rescue. A losing venue must remain solvent on its own even when the other venue gains. Existing isolated funding-allocation semantics differ from Bybit's documented available-balance-first deduction; copying an isolated configuration would misstate that venue. Cross mode avoids that particular mismatch, without replicating all live tier/ADL/risk behavior.

Match underlying quantity on the common native lot grid, not superficially equal dollar notionals. Both 1000PEPE products use bundle prices/quantities; their currently observed common step is 100 native bundles. Check each venue's minima/maxima separately. Prices differ, so equal underlying quantity does not make dollar exposure zero. Record each wallet, native residual quantity, basis exposure, marks, funding payment and leg PnL separately.

Funding cash for every actually held venue position is `-signed_native_quantity * venue_settlement_mark * actual_rate`. Include each asynchronous event independently; never collapse it into a synthetic simultaneous differential payment or credit the forecast itself. Bybit's missing exact settlement mark remains unresolved. A minute mark-open proxy would require an explicitly frozen approximation and valuation/availability sensitivity; no timing precision or proxy choice is selected by this planning review. If exact payments are required, obtain authentic settlement valuation evidence before proceeding.

Both entries and both exits pay their own taker fees/slippage; use prior completed venue volume and independent delayed/partial fills. Current fee/rule observations must be labeled historical approximations. Bybit currently publishes a non-VIP taker rate of 0.055%; that does not prove an account's entire-year fee tier. [Official contract FAQ](https://www.bybit.com/en/help-center/article/FAQ-USDT-Perpetual-and-Expiry-Contracts).

Compare a conservative forward carry budget based only on past yields with the **unnetted two-venue round-trip cost**, funding reversal/basis allowance and cleanup risk in consistent dollar units. Current mark notionals can differ even with matched quantity. Do not count favorable future basis movement or future funding as an admission credit. Freeze doubled-fee/slippage, additional-delay and reduced-participation stress before outcomes. One immutable pair target, bounded leg timeout and persistent reduce-only cleanup are necessary; no atomic fill, fabricated close or free collateral movement is available. Genuine terminal positions remain marked with an indicative exit reserve.

Later phases may update only the prescribed strictly past rolling history, not policy constants.

The bounded Bybit probes establish endpoint availability, **not** full-year minute/mark coverage or settlement completeness. A complete source packet must preserve raw responses, pagination/bounds, conflicting-duplicate rejection, exact native identities and independently published marks. Missing funding events cannot be interpolated; missing candles cannot be traded through. Historical receipt/schedule/mark gaps remain explicit source limitations.

Before implementation, require complete acquisition/validation and explicit resolution or disclosure of those gaps, then one source/runtime/protocol freeze and synthetic tests for variable intervals, delayed receipts, expiry, same-clock events, quantity normalization, venue-local insolvency and failed legs. Final publication needs actual cash/quantity/funding reconciliation and deterministic summary reconstruction. No test or bootstrap can restore untouched evaluation after this year has been repeatedly inspected, and this planning memo does not increase the tested count or promise a profitable result.
