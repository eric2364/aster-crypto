# Positioning hypothesis: confirmed position-unwind rebound

Research date: **6 October 2026**. This document specifies one economic hypothesis before its numerical outcomes. It does not inspect historical feature values, event frequencies, trading results or threshold performance. It reads the existing [source feasibility](next_positioning_feasibility.md) and [research decision](next_factor_decision.md), whose current checkpoint is **0/27 historical hurdle passes**. The fixed observation year remains 2025-10-05 inclusive to 2026-10-05 exclusive, UTC. Reusing this examined year is exploratory research, not prospective preregistration or untouched confirmation.

The recommended family is **long/cash after a completed price decline accompanied by a contraction in reported native open-interest quantity, followed by a completed price rebound and one observed interval without further OI contraction**. This is a *position-unwind proxy*. It must never be described as detection of actual forced liquidations or an established profitable liquidation strategy.

These sources were checked through primary publication records, author manuscripts or primary indexed abstracts. Full-text fetching of the Harvard PDF and some NBER pages was unavailable in the browsing service; their primary-source indexed abstracts supported the narrow statements above. No secondary trading article supplies the rule or any claim of profitability. No direct passage is quoted.

**Inference, not a published result:** after a price decline with fewer outstanding positions, a later observed rebound could indicate that transient selling pressure is easing and that liquidity provision earns a premium. A simultaneous pause in reported OI contraction is a weak additional description of that state. Information-driven repricing, voluntary hedge closure, delayed reporting, contract migration and continuing deleveraging remain competing explanations. The literature does not choose a profitable sign, threshold or horizon for this dataset.

## Why this mechanism, rather than another positioning story

| Alternative | Assessment before outcomes |
|---|---|
| Buy immediately when price and OI fall | A coherent pressure/exhaustion conjecture, but it enters while the documented cascade mechanism may still be active. Prefer waiting for observed rebound confirmation. |
| Short price/OI contractions as continuing stress | Also economically plausible. Testing both signs and selecting the winner would add search; this proposal fixes the rebound direction instead. |
| Follow price increases accompanied by OI growth | New positions could reflect speculation, hedging or informed demand. This would overlap existing trend families, and matched OI supplies no standalone direction. |
| Fade a high account long/short ratio | Account counts are not aggregate directional exposure. A margin-ranked participant sample is not verified informed capital, and positions may hedge exposures elsewhere. The interpretation is weaker than a within-contract quantity contraction. |
| Trade inferred liquidations or squeeze events | Unsupported without authentic event identities, side semantics and historical receipt coverage. Neither a candle wick nor OI contraction supplies that evidence. |

The distinct input is the **stock of outstanding reported positions**. The trade still contains a reversal component and is related to previous pressure/reversal hypotheses; it is not an independent edge merely because a new field is added. A favorable result would need to show that its OI condition has descriptive value beyond the same price confirmation. Any later price-only ablation is an additional disclosed analysis, not permission to choose the better rule after seeing results.

## Fixed candidate specification for the pre-outcome protocol

The following adopts the root's provisional design. All numbers are **transparent engineering heuristics**, not paper estimates or calibrated historical quantiles. One family is proposed; its implementation and source seal must record the final rule before any outcome run.

| Component | Fixed proposal |
|---|---|
| Instruments | Existing BTC, ETH, DOGE, native **1000PEPE**, SOL, XRP, LINK and ADA linear perpetuals; no expanded or outcome-selected universe. |
| Shock state | Latest usable reported native OI quantity change over one hour **≤ −2%**, and completed same-instrument trade-price change over one hour **≤ −1.5%**. |
| Rebound confirmation | Completed trade-price change over 15 minutes **≥ +0.3%**. This is an observed past rebound, not a promised forward profit. |
| OI pause condition | Latest usable native OI quantity change over one five-minute interval **≥ 0**. Both endpoints must satisfy the availability rule and exact coverage requirements. |
| Decisions and direction | Every five minutes, long or cash only. If several instruments qualify while flat, use the predeclared universe order, never the largest observed move or highest later return. One active asset account-wide. |
| Entry size | Fixed quantity from **10% of current equity** entry notional, within the existing declared collateral, leverage, minimum-size and fill rules. No minute-by-minute resizing and no averaging down. |
| Exit | At the first decision observing a **2% adverse completed-price move from actual entry execution**, or at four hours maximum holding, whichever occurs first; execute subsequently under the runner's order/fill rules. A gap can exceed 2%; a stop signal is not a guaranteed execution price. |
| Rearming | One hour flat cooldown after exit. Existing positions are not reentered or enlarged because another overlapping shock window qualifies. |
| Baseline execution | **5 bps taker fee + 8 bps slippage per side**; actual historical funding and all account events remain separate. No assumed maker rebate or guaranteed maker fill. |
| Cost stress | **10 bps fee + 16 bps slippage per side**, with the same sizing rule and economic logic; actual fills and admitted quantities may differ. Higher observed market impact is not ruled out by this stress assumption. |

The OI pause is not a theoretically necessary condition for a price-confirmed unwind proxy. It is included to make the phrase *observed contraction has paused* literal. A single nonnegative observation is noisy: it does not demonstrate cessation of selling, reduction of leverage, liquidity recovery or exhaustion. Requiring a sustained plateau might also be plausible, but introducing several plateau windows and selecting on this year would create another search. Freeze this simple condition once and preserve zero-activity as well as losing results.

The baseline fee/slippage floor is **26 bps round trip**, and stress is **52 bps**, before funding. The 30 bps rebound threshold describes a move **already completed before entry**; it is not a cost budget available to the new position. A four-hour horizon reduces reliance on a seconds-long microstructure effect but is itself unvalidated. Attractive gross event moves can coexist with negative executable returns after delayed confirmation, fees and adverse selection. A candle-based slippage model cannot verify stressed depth, queue priority or market impact, particularly for DOGE and 1000PEPE. Slippage already embedded in execution prices must not be subtracted a second time from P&L.

## Availability, native units and identification limits

Use `sum_open_interest` **quantity**, not quote-valued `sum_open_interest_value`, for contraction. Under a stable multiplier, reported value combines quantity and valuation price; falling value alone cannot distinguish position closure from a price fall. A contract's percentage quantity change is unchanged by a constant bundle multiplier, but price, notional and execution quantity must all use its native contract convention. Retain `1000PEPEUSDT` prices and quantities without an additional spot-PEPE conversion.

Binance distinguishes total OI from its value, offers five-minute REST history only for the latest month, and documents REST period-end timestamps. Its account ratios refer to a specified population, including the highest-margin-balance users for top-trader ratios. These definitions do not establish archive content clocks or directional distress. [Official USDⓈ-M market-data definitions](https://developers.binance.com/en/docs/catalog/core-trading-derivatives-trading-usd-s-m-futures/api/rest-api/market-data).

For each assumed-UTC archive label `u`, baseline modeled availability is **u + 6 minutes**, and the fixed delay stress is **u + 11 minutes**. Require `available_at < decision_time`, not equality. Completed minute trade-price inputs must satisfy **bar_start + 2 minutes ≤ decision_time**. Align through backward as-of matching only, retain each source timestamp, reject conflicting duplicates, and reset feature windows after missing five-minute observations. Do not interpolate positioning to manufacture minute observations or bridge gaps. Window endpoints and intervening observations must meet the declared coverage policy; no pre-year seed is imported.

These lags cover a modeled interval-label ambiguity and processing allowance; they do **not** prove contemporaneous receipt. Daily archives are published subsequently and may be revised. The assumed minute availability is therefore a hypothetical historical live-feed reconstruction, not the actual archive download's availability. Checksum agreement and complete coverage establish current byte integrity; they do not identify original live delivery or an unrevised historical vintage. Longer-delay sensitivity cannot repair an unknown revision. [Binance archive publication and update policy](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data). The independent [dataset audit](positioning_dataset_audit.md) and [source memo](next_positioning_feasibility.md) remain controlling evidence, including unresolved field-unit and content-clock semantics.

Outstanding OI is matched across sides. A quantity contraction does not identify whether long or short traders were distressed, voluntary versus forced closure, aggregate leverage, an individual trader's liquidation, or a directionally informed position. A correct parser cannot solve these identification problems. Changes in reporting convention can mimic economic shocks; do not mix venue definitions or silently repair a discontinuity with a return-driven correction.

## Failure criteria and permissible interpretation

Freeze source/input/code hashes, exact phases, all financial gates, trial counting, cost/delay stress and no-trade rules before outcomes. Independent tests should perturb future price and positioning rows, delay individual publications, insert missing/duplicate clocks and swap native identities. Signals at earlier decisions must remain unchanged by future edits; unavailable, conflicting or missing evidence must prevent entries. Independently reconstruct fills, funding, equity and native quantities.

Apply the existing cumulative multiple-search policy, including this additional economic family. Do not present one asset, one phase or one favorable stress as the family result, lower thresholds because trade count is small, remove a losing symbol, flip the economic direction or relax costs after seeing outcomes. Correlated events and common market shocks are not independent minute samples. Report event/trade counts and their concentration, all inactive sleeves, realistic blocked resampling and the existing account-level hurdles.

A failed rule falsifies this **fixed operational version on this examined year**, not all liquidity-pressure theories. A pass would be a historically exploratory hurdle pass conditional on uncertain source timing and the chosen execution model. It would not prove forced liquidation identification, establish four independent winners, demonstrate deployable capacity or remove the need for untouched subsequent data and prospective receipt logging. No successful strategy is claimed by this research memo.
