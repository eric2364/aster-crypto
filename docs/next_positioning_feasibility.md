# Positioning data feasibility — 6 October 2026

**A five-minute open-interest contraction study is a feasible next data-acquisition option; a historical forced-liquidation strategy is not yet supported by a verified yearly event corpus.** Open interest supplies position-stock information distinct from price, settled funding and aggressive trade flow. Its predictive value after publication delay and execution costs remains untested. This memo adds no fitted model, signal, candidate or successful example: the existing result remains **0/26**. The only contemplated window is 2025-10-05 00:00 UTC inclusive through 2026-10-05 00:00 UTC exclusive, on the existing BTC, ETH, DOGE, PEPE, SOL, XRP, LINK and ADA universe.

## Actual offline sample audit

The existing root-owned probes downloaded six days per native contract: **2025-10-05, 2026-04-05, 2026-06-24/25/26 and 2026-10-04**. Independent ZIP/CSV inspection, without calling the probe's parser, verified all **48** official checksums, compressed and decompressed hashes, exact native symbols and this exact schema:

```
create_time,symbol,sum_open_interest,sum_open_interest_value,
count_toptrader_long_short_ratio,sum_toptrader_long_short_ratio,
count_long_short_ratio,sum_taker_long_short_vol_ratio
```

There are **13,824 sampled rows and 551,908 compressed bytes**. Each file's timestamp set contains exactly 288 unique assumed-UTC five-minute labels, 00:00 through 23:55. All eight last-day files are physically **unordered**; the other 40 samples are ordered. Physical first/last rows therefore do not represent minimum/maximum time, and file order is not publication sequence. Preserve this diagnostic, reject duplicate labels, then sort for normalization. The 16 adjacent June 24→25 and June 25→26 boundaries have no repeated labels and a sorted five-minute spacing. No OI quantities, ratios, price returns, yields or trading outcomes were analyzed.

[Independent machine-readable review](positioning_probe_review.json) binds the 48 archive/checksum/CSV hashes, schemas, clocks, ordering and both immutable probe receipts. [Initial receipt](positioning_archive_probe.json) and [transition receipt](positioning_transition_probe.json) remain unchanged. Their `verified_archive` status establishes bounded integrity/schema/native clock coverage, not full-year coverage, finite economic values or an execution-ready series. The eventual collector needs stricter numeric/schema validation and explicit gap failure rules.

## Timestamp and vintage hazards

Archive `create_time` strings are offset-free: assigning UTC is an explicit parser assumption. REST's documented period-end timestamp does **not** establish that an archive label is a snapshot time or original receipt. A [community issue](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data/issues/491) alleges a June 25 end-to-start labeling change. It is not an official confirmation. Our currently retrieved June 24–26 samples do **not** reproduce its alleged duplicate boundary or old June 24 label range; label integrity cannot determine when their economic content was observed. Do not silently impose the allegation's historical shift, or infer it false from today's revised files.

Some archive `Last-Modified` headers substantially postdate their observation days. Binance explicitly permits later archive updates. Checksums prove these retrieved bytes match the currently published files, not that the same vintage was available to a trader at the time. HTTP retrieval times are today's receipts. Adding a lag does not turn backfilled data into a point-in-time archive. [Official checksum/update policy](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data).

Minute execution can coexist with **five-minute predictor updates**: a complete year would contain 105,120 five-minute observations per asset, or 840,960 across eight, not 525,600 independent positioning updates per asset. A prospective protocol should first resolve content-clock semantics where possible. If ambiguity remains, a declared conservative assumption such as label + one full five-minute interval + one processing minute can cover the proposed start-versus-end ambiguity, with a longer-delay sensitivity. It cannot bound unknown delivery latency or revisions. Use strictly prior available observations, no interpolation, bounded stale carry and rolling-window reset after missing observations. Preserve original labels and assumed availability separately. No pre-year baseline or future close can be imported to complete the first observation.

## Economic interpretation and primary evidence

OI contraction is a **position-unwind proxy**, not a liquidation count. Total matched open positions do not identify which side was distressed, leverage, voluntary versus forced closure, or an imminent rebound. Quote-valued OI can fall mechanically when price falls; under a constant multiplier, value = native quantity × valuation price. Use quantity changes for contraction and disclose the valuation component separately. For `1000PEPEUSDT`, retain native bundle units; neither its price nor quantity should receive a second spot-PEPE conversion. Long/short account ratios describe sampled account populations, not universal directional inventory.

Cheng, Deng, Wang and Yu's [Liquidation, Leverage and Optimal Margin in Bitcoin Futures Markets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.04591) documents substantial forced liquidation and fat-tail margin risk in BitMEX perpetual BTC. It motivates distinct leverage/position-risk information, but does not establish a profitable delayed Binance altcoin predictor. Lehar and Parlour's [BIS study of Compound/Aave liquidations](https://www.bis.org/publications/working-paper-1062-systemic-fragility-decentralised-markets) finds temporary **and permanent** price impact with contagion feedback. Forced selling need not exhaust immediately; the DeFi mechanism also differs from centralized linear perpetuals. These are mechanisms and external-validity limits, not evidence that DOGE/PEPE minute reversal passes our costs.

Actual liquidation data would require authentic event and local receipt timestamps, side semantics, outage coverage and publisher sampling rules. Bybit `Buy` means a **long position** was liquidated, and its price field is bankruptcy price, not necessarily executable trade price. Do not manufacture liquidation events from candle wicks, OI decreases or funding observations. [Bybit message definitions](https://bybit-exchange.github.io/docs/v5/websocket/public/all-liquidation).

## Recommended next action

Collect and independently seal the full-year **Binance metrics** archive first, preserving raw order, published checksums, retrieval metadata, native identity, label semantics and missingness. This is justified as acquisition of a distinct possible input, not acquisition of demonstrated alpha. A complete eight-asset daily route requires 2,920 ZIP/checksum pairs; the sample size does not justify a disk estimate. Reject conflicting duplicates, invalid quantities and out-of-window labels; report gaps instead of filling them. A timing/field-content audit should precede signal implementation.

Only then approve one economically explicit family around the delayed response to completed price moves accompanied by native OI contraction. Exhaustion/reversal and continuing stress are competing hypotheses; counting their threshold variants as independent discoveries would misstate evidence. Freeze the economic direction, cost budget, timing assumptions, training-only admissibility, no-trade rules and all failure gates before numerical outcomes. Existing minute trade/mark/funding execution can be reused without changing frozen prior studies. The year has already been examined for 26 families, so this remains exploratory and cannot prove absence of overfitting or promise four to five robust examples. Lack of verified historical liquidation receipts limits that particular mechanism; it does not exhaust price-only or other research possibilities.
