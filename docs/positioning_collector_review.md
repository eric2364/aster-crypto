# Independent positioning collector review

2026-10-06. Review of the new data-only `examples/positioning/collect_archives.py`; no strategy fit, economic feature, historical price scan, full collection or Engine run was performed by this reviewer. The earlier sampled source probe remains unchanged. Full-year availability is not established by sampled archives or these synthetic tests.

The collector requests native Binance USD-M daily metrics ZIPs and their matching official CHECKSUM files. It preserves raw responses, request identities, retrieval clocks/headers, hashes, CSV schema, original source label/day/order, and native 1000PEPE quantities. It normalizes only by chronological sorting. NaNs, nonpositive required fields, schema drift, wrong native IDs, missing/off-grid/duplicate labels and incompatible cross-day clock sets fail closed. It never interpolates, shifts period labels or invents historical delivery availability.

The parser accepts either a complete start-labelled or end-labelled daily five-minute grid while recording which label set is actually observed. The full-year assembler still requires the exact declared in-year grid and rejects boundary duplicates or missing labels. Consequently a year of end-labelled files or a start/end-label transition can intentionally block completion; acceptance at the daily parser is not a promise that a semantically reconciled annual dataset exists. Arbitrary shifting, dropping the conflicting row or inserting a missing initial row would need a separately justified version, not an automatic repair.

Independent tests use official-shaped synthetic ZIPs with 288 known observations. They verify exact values, chronological reconstruction, retained original row indices and raw labels, native identity and absent `available_at`; missing, duplicate, extra, off-grid, out-of-day and wrong-native records; NaN/infinite/negative values; checksum filename and content; and an end-labelled cross-day duplicate with conflicting values. A complete synthetic one-day collection is replayed and restarted with network access prohibited, confirming all evidence bytes remain unchanged.

Adversarial verification updates the outer seal after altering a normalized value, a manifest's historical-receipt claim, or a raw receipt's URL, UTC clock, diagnostic or content hash. Independent raw reparse and manifest reconstruction still reject those changes. Additional tests reject seal path traversal before opening arbitrary files, runtime drift and incomplete raw-page resume without download or overwrite. The archived collector bytes, source/runtime seal, exact normalized filename map and raw receipt namespace are checked before certification. These hashes provide reproducible consistency, not authentication of a maliciously rewritten entire source package.

## Temporal and economic limits

An open [community issue in Binance's official repository](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data/issues/491) reports a possible June 2026 metrics label/content convention change. This is an unconfirmed user report, not authoritative exchange confirmation. Its claimed earlier label range differs from the October 2025 and April 2026 sampled archives already cached here. Neither that disagreement nor a complete five-minute label grid establishes when the underlying observations became available. The collector correctly supplies no predictor availability clock. Any future strategy requires an explicit conservative publication assumption and independent timing review, including possible revised archives and future-content contamination; it must not infer historical receipts from today's download times. [Official archive documentation](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data).

Open-interest value can change with price even when quantity is constant. The six numeric series have distinct meanings; top-trader positions/account ratios and taker volume are not interchangeable or a historical order book. Source-native OI quantities and 1000PEPE identities are preserved without a claimed historical unit/contract guarantee. There is no evidence here of profitable predictors or complete historical receipt timing.
