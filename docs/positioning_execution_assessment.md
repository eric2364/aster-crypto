# Prospective positioning-event execution assessment

6 October 2026. **A long/cash, single-active-instrument unwind-and-recovery hypothesis is implementable with the existing eight-native Engine and one common cross-margin wallet.** It is economically different from candidate27's weekly four-leg funding rank: entry concerns an intraday contraction in outstanding quantity positions followed by price recovery; actual funding remains an account cash flow, not a selection variable. This is an untested possible candidate28, not a successful strategy or an approved final protocol. This review reads source/schema/design documents only; it generates no historical features, event frequencies, parameter fit or strategy returns.

## Hypothesis and evidence boundary

A contraction in native quantity OI during a decline can indicate closing positions. A subsequent recovery may signal exhaustion of temporary selling pressure. It can equally reflect voluntary de-risking, continuing informed selling, a change in unit definition, or backfilled data. OI does not identify liquidated longs, leverage, trader profitability or which party initiated closing. A confirmed rebound is a price condition, not proof of the proposed causal story.

[Liquidation, Leverage and Optimal Margin in Bitcoin Futures Markets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.04591) provides primary evidence of liquidation and margin risk in BitMEX BTC, not a profitable delayed Binance altcoin reversal rule. The [existing positioning feasibility review](next_positioning_feasibility.md) and [flow evidence review](next_flow_evidence.md) explain why temporary pressure does not guarantee cost-covering reversal. There is enough distinction to justify one prospective hypothesis, not four or five presumed winners. Do not reverse a known losing flow policy or add a profitability-selected asset filter.

Use `sum_open_interest`, not `sum_open_interest_value`: the latter also changes mechanically with price. Preserve native `1000PEPEUSDT` quantities and prices; percentage OI changes are dimensionless within a consistently defined series, not permission to convert quantities into spot PEPE or assert stable historical contract semantics. Account/position ratios and `sum_taker_long_short_vol_ratio` should remain unused in this first mechanism: their populations and period-content semantics add unresolved interpretation. Authentic minute taker flow is available elsewhere but is unnecessary for this hypothesis.

The full [data-only audit](positioning_dataset_audit.md) establishes today's 840,960 sorted observations and raw identity/order/checksum reconstruction. It does **not** certify original information time, publication latency, an unrevised historical vintage or constant historical units. Binance expressly allows archive revisions in its [official public-data documentation](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data). A delay assumption cannot repair arbitrary revisions; the outcome must retain that limitation.

## Minimal proposed rule for root review

Root's authoritative [draft protocol](../examples/positioning_research/proposed_protocol.md) now specifies: every five-minute review, require native quantity OI down at least 2% over one hour, latest five-minute quantity change nonnegative, price down at least 1.5% over one hour, and a positive 15-minute closed-price return of at least 0.3%. Buy only after these observations are available. These round thresholds are prospective choices, not literature estimates or fitted cutoffs. No threshold neighbors, funding-rank screen, account/taker ratio or asset profitability filter is included. Nonnegative latest OI change is a weak stabilization proxy, not proof that liquidation or selling has finished.

Evaluate all eight assets at the same callback, choose the first currently eligible asset in the fixed `SYMBOLS` order, and log/discard the others. No queued proposal survives entry, exit, cooldown or failed admission. After cooldown, a new review of current causal inputs can admit a still-eligible condition; the adopted packet does not impose the false-to-true event-edge rule considered during this assessment. Refused admission receives the same one-hour cooldown rather than an automatic retry on every review. The priority-order bias and this persistence behavior must remain disclosed.

The approved lifecycle uses one immutable entry, maximum four hours from the **actual final entry fill**, and a 2% adverse closed-price exit relative to actual entry VWAP. No take-profit or estimated funding-profit admission is used. A price stop is a request for a later executable exit, not a guaranteed loss cap. Monitor stop, stale/invalid input and account risk each minute, including partial entry. Partial entry that remains incomplete after its activation-plus-five-minute deadline is canceled and cleaned up; it cannot become completed participation. All genuinely flat, resolved closes and failure cleanup start a one-hour cooldown. These replace the first-fill horizon and 24-hour failure-cooldown alternatives considered earlier; the changes occur before any outcomes.

## Exact causal clocks

Keep the complete original raw label `t`, source-day identity and original row order. Propose `metric_available_at=t+6 minutes`, with a separately declared sensitivity at `t+11 minutes`. At completed-minute decision `d`, use only observations with **available_at < d**. For example a raw 00:00 label is first eligible at 00:07 baseline or 00:12 sensitivity. This covers a possible one-five-minute-period content ambiguity plus processing assumption; it is not an original receipt and does not bound undocumented archive revision or delivery delays. The alleged historical label change remains unconfirmed, as documented in the source reviews.

Compute OI change between the exact latest eligible raw label and its exact one-hour predecessor, requiring all 13 five-minute labels in that interval. Do not nearest-match across a gap, interpolate, duplicate labels or cross the year start for warmup. Require the latest assumed availability to be no more than five minutes old at decision; keep unavailable/invalid features explicit rather than minute-fill observations. Carrying a known five-minute observation for a bounded causal decision is distinct from fabricating new OI measurements.

For minute bars with start `u`, the closed-price observation becomes usable at `u+2 minutes`; price availability may be **<= d**. Price one-hour and 15-minute returns use exact predecessors of the latest eligible price endpoint and complete intervening minute grids. Do not use the decision bar's upcoming open, high, low or close. Metrics and prices need not share the same physical endpoint: retain both source endpoints and availability clocks so this intentional asynchronous comparison is visible. Baseline and delayed-metric sensitivity retain the same price clock and model constants. Only [2025-10-05,2026-10-05) sources are allowed; phase warmup can use earlier observations inside that year, never earlier data.

## Common wallet and actual order lifecycle

Use one actual 100,000 USDT Binance cross wallet, leverage one, eight native Instruments, and at most one active instrument. Recommend a **10% of then-current equity** entry-notional budget with exact native-lot flooring. Starting at the existing 25% position cap would leave essentially no headroom for favorable marked-price drift; a cap breach could abort a winning position immediately. Include native minimum quantity/notional, entry fee/slippage cash allowance, positive available margin, aggregate core admission and historical-current-rule approximation. No hypothetical future funding, exit cash or OI-sized quantity enters admission.

Retain account gross cap 50%, individual marked-position cap 25%, maximum 80% margin utilization, positive free margin, maintenance/close-reserve and account-equity controls at every minute. Baseline uses fixed 5 bps fees /8 bps adverse slippage, stress 10 /16 bps, one-minute order delay and half prior-volume participation. Approximate roundtrip taker costs are 26 and 52 bps before funding/price-risk effects; delayed recovery may be too small or already exhausted. There is no cost-free maker assumption.

A small centralized state machine is enough: `IDLE → ENTERING → HOLDING → EXITING → COOLDOWN`, with latched `ABORTING/BLOCKED` and terminal mode. Keep global state despite a single leg: eight independent sleeves can still issue conflicting account actions. Entry freezes symbol/direction/native target/event identity/cash admission and one GTC market order. Deadline is original submission + configured activation delay + five minutes. Never cancel/reissue it merely because a new feature or ranking arrives. Completion is actual target attainment plus order resolution; record actual last-fill time separately from the later callback that observes completion. A partial target is actual exposed capital, not completed participation. Full entry records live in `completed_entries`; breadth/activity gates must use `completed_episodes`, which requires that full entry later actually closes flat with orders resolved. The compatibility alias `completed_baskets` names completed episodes, not open entries.

Before normal exit, cancel unresolved opening orders; size one reduce-only GTC order from the actual remaining quantity. Preserve valid exits and original deadlines across repeated callbacks. A terminal/rejected/cancelled-but-unmet order, deadline, overshoot or actual liquidation abandons the signal and latches failure once. Cancel opening intents, submit only permitted actual reduce-only cleanup, keep valid cleanup live and record its immutable deadline. Rejected/cancelled cleanup is blocked, not automatically restored each minute. Never claim flat with nonzero dust or because the core positions table omits a tiny balance. No new action uses a stale proposal, a synthetic fill, expected exit proceeds or an inferred zero position.

At `phase_stop−10 minutes`, latch terminal mode, clear entry eligibility, cancel opens, and retain existing valid reduce-only exits/cleanup. Submit actual remaining exits only if necessary. Disallow entry at or after phase stop minus four hours and ten minutes. At END retain genuine marks, sourced funding and residual inventory; fail a terminal-resolution gate for any actual nonzero quantity or live order. No after-END bar or manufactured cash closure is allowed. Funding predictors are unnecessary, but payment cash must use actual held quantities at each sourced settlement, including a sourced boundary event under existing valuation policy.

## Implementation and adversarial verification

No Engine extension, second wallet, beta fit, synthetic hedge or new collector is required. Add a small candidate-specific pure feature module and coordinator; keep previous frozen sources untouched. Reuse the existing eight-native phase-input/export primitives only under explicit transitive source hashes. One fixed five-phase study and a declared availability sensitivity can use the same runner pattern; any extra trajectory must be counted and frozen rather than smuggled in as debugging. Avoid a new general portfolio framework or bespoke plotting/accounting stack. Adapt independent financial replay and add a small separately scoped policy verifier; do not treat recorded cash conservation as proof of all omitted signals.

Before historical features, synthetic tests should cover: metric raw-label+6/+11 strict boundaries; exact hour predecessors and gaps; price+2-minute visibility; prefix/future invariance; unchanged PEPE native percentage arithmetic; persistent versus newly eligible event edges; simultaneous eight-asset tie handling; admission rejection consuming rather than queueing an event; a delayed partial fill reaching stop/time before completion; a new signal while entry/exit is pending; no stale reentry after cleanup; final-entry-fill holding age; exact fresh-wallet cash/lot/minimum admission; favorable mark drift and maintenance limits; rejection/cancellation with no deadline extension; valid cleanup retained on terminal; subminimum dust; liquidation while already flat; and strictly no fabricated final fills.

The prospective decision is therefore **feasible with material causal-data limitations**, conditional on final source review and freezing the stated thresholds, clocks, allocation, exits and activity/breadth gates before inspecting event counts. Eight eligible names do not guarantee DOGE/PEPE trades or four profitable assets. A cash-only or sparsely triggered result remains disclosed; no hurdle is weakened to meet the four-or-five-success objective.
