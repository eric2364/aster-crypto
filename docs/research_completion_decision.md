**Correct the residual-margin model and rerun identical derivative hypotheses before adding candidates. The requested four or five robust successes have not been established.** The published original summaries contain zero passes, but the sixteen derivative policy labels are now provisional because a declared collateral-allocation convention affects their paths and liquidation gates. The eight spot policies are unaffected by this partial-derivative-reserve correction.

The original convention proportionally released collateral and then retained the entire closed-leg realized PnL minus exit fee in the residual collateral. Cash correctly recorded these amounts, so all wallet conservation checks could pass. A near-full losing exit nevertheless depleted a tiny residual reserve and induced liquidation. This is a flaw in the chosen residual-allocation model, not evidence that cash fees or PnL were double-counted. The correction adopts proportional reserve release; it does not establish exact historical Binance mechanics or amend uncertain historical fee/rule assumptions.

A zero-fee handcase demonstrates the distinction: one unit long at100 with leverage1 reserves100. Closing0.95 at95 realizes−4.75 into cash. The remaining0.05 has proportional collateral5 and unrealized−0.25, exceeding maintenance0.02375. The old allocation instead leaves collateral0.25 and liquidates the residual. Profitable partial exits can distort remaining margin in the opposite direction.

An independent replay scoped all1,000 original derivative executions. Of77 recorded liquidation events,54 have healthy residual margin under proportional-only release **conditional on the same recorded fills**:12/35 carry,17/17 signed and25/25 external. The remaining23 carry events remain below maintenance under that conditional calculation; their risks must remain explicit. This replay is not a corrected strategy backtest: preventing one liquidation changes subsequent inventory, equity, sizing and fills. All original records remain preserved. [Exact event states](original_derivative_liquidation_states.csv) and [compact scope](original_derivative_liquidation_review.json) document the distinction.

Other results warrant caution, rather than another immediate parameter search. Costs consume much of several small signals; the funding-crowding evaluation gain was only$52.84 and three PEPE trades, with seven idle sleeves. CPI has eleven unique announcements across the entire year, not eighty-eight independent events. Premium fade generated no evaluation trades. Closed-trade gross-profit/loss tables alone are unsuitable for attribution: include terminal unrealized inventory and actual funding, subtract fees and liquidation charges, and keep slippage embedded in execution-price PnL. No further systemic cash-accounting flaw was established by this review.

Multiple-testing statistics cannot reverse adaptive reuse or prove absence of overfitting.
