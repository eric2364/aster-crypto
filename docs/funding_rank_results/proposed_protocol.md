# Draft economic packet: joint cross-sectional perpetual funding rank

**Candidate 27, proposed and not yet preregistered or executed.** This is one funding-relative-value portfolio hypothesis on a reused historical year, economically related to prior funding crowding fades. None of the prior 26 definitions implemented this different-underlying funding-ranked basket in a joint wallet. All prior sources, protocols, outcomes and failure gates remain preserved. This draft must receive economic and independent execution review before code/input freeze and numerical outputs; it supplies no fitted parameters or observed returns.

## Mechanism, universe and clocks

Buy the two lowest past-realized funding intensities and sell the two highest across BTC, ETH, DOGE, PEPE, SOL, XRP, LINK and ADA Binance native USDT linear perpetuals. The hypothesis combines persistence of relative funding payments with risk-bearing cross-sectional price returns. It is **dollar-balanced at entry, not market-neutral, beta-neutral or an arbitrage**. No fitted beta hedge, OI filter, momentum confirmation, future funding announcement or copied external-paper winner is used.

Use only `[2025-10-05T00:00Z,2026-10-05T00:00Z)`, with actual native minute trade/mark bars and actual settled funding. Native PEPE is `1000PEPEUSDT`: prices and quantities remain per 1,000-PEPE bundle; different-underlying quantities are never matched by contract count. The first six months supply causal history/source quality only, with no fitting, performance selection or parameter grid. All engineering defaults below are prospective choices for a slow, interpretable experiment, not estimated optimal values or literature-derived profit guarantees.

**New basket proposals and rank selection occur only on Sunday at 00:05 UTC.** A flat fresh-wallet fold waits for its first such clock; there is no special proposal at fold start or Monday catch-up. Actual old-basket close orders and fresh entry sizing/submission may occur on later callbacks before that immutable proposal expires at00:20, as specified below; ranks are never recomputed during this window. Daily 00:05 UTC reviews can close an existing basket but cannot change its ranks or open another. Minute callbacks monitor execution, hard holding age and account risk; intentionally absent nondecision funding features must not trigger an exit. The weekly schedule reduces turnover relative to daily ranking and makes its cost/holding interpretation explicit.

## Exact causal funding predictor

Reuse the frozen `prepare_funding` / `yield_at` semantics from [cross-venue signals](../cross_venue/signals.py) without changing that module. Prepare each Binance native stream separately; preserve its source fingerprint. At time `t` use only records whose availability is **strictly before t**, rebuilding predecessors from that known subset, including out-of-order receipt. Explicit original receipt must be at/after settlement and receives one further minute processing lag. Without receipt, settlement+one minute is an assumed availability, never today's download time. No outside-year predecessor is supplied.

For adjacent known settlements ending at `s_j`, let actual elapsed hours be `d_j`. Recognize durations only within five seconds of `{1,2,4,8}` hours, but retain actual durations in calculations. In `[t−168h,t]`, let `w_j` be the positive overlap of that completed interval. Yield per hour is `u_i(t)=Σ(w_j×rate_j/d_j)/Σw_j`. The first sourced settlement contributes no interval. Require at least 90% covered hours; no invalid overlapping interval; one common recognized cadence label among overlapping intervals; and latest-settlement age no greater than its recognized actual elapsed interval+10 minutes. Missing every other payment can still imitate regular cadence: raw-source completeness and unresolved-omission failure are separately required. Genuine cadence transitions are conservatively suppressed, not repaired.

Every entry requires **all eight** valid finite intensities and available positive native prices. Sort ascending by intensity, breaking ties by the original fixed universe order `BTC, ETH, DOGE, PEPE, SOL, XRP, LINK, ADA`. Select first two longs and last two shorts. Require strictly positive `gap=mean(u_shorts)−mean(u_longs)`; ties yielding zero gap leave cash. This unweighted rank gap is largely implied by sorting except degeneracy; it is not an empirical carry-profit screen. Rounded actual quantities, different notionals/marks and subsequent price changes can produce nonpositive notional-weighted predicted funding despite positive rank gap. Report that diagnostic honestly without modifying admission or promising positive weighted carry or forward total P&L. No absolute forecast-carry-versus-cost admission screen applies: that would impose a different funding-arbitrage hypothesis and ignore risk-priced price P&L. Disclose the retained-quantity indicative 168h funding forecast and conservative unnetted roundtrip costs separately; neither is a future cash guarantee or an outcome-selected entry threshold.

At each daily review, close the **whole retained basket** if any of its four funding inputs is invalid/stale or its original short-mean minus long-mean intensity is nonpositive. Invalid unused assets do not retroactively alter a held basket; all eight must be valid at the next proposed entry. Otherwise native quantities remain fixed until exit. Prices use completed trade close available at source start+60s completion+60s processing, with at most two-minute staleness and no interpolation. A future/unavailable record cannot change past admission, rank, quantity or no-trade state.

## Actual capital, costs and collateral

One actual eight-instrument Engine run, one initial **100,000 USDT Binance cash wallet**, cash-only **cross margin**, leverage1, account `max_gross_leverage=.5`, `max_position_pct=.25`. No borrowing, spot inventory collateral, transfers, capital replenishment or funding-only synthetic equity. Gross initial basket budget is **25% of then-current account equity**, split equally across four legs: **6,250 USDT per leg at 100,000 equity**, two long and two short (12,500 per side). Idle budgets remain cash.

For each selected leg, floor `(.25×equity/4)/known_native_price` to its own exact native lot grid. Check native lot grids, opening quantity/notional minima, instrument/configured leverage and aggregate configured position/gross/margin limits before submitting any leg. The Instrument model has no `max_quantity` field: this packet cannot claim enforcement of a separate exchange opening-quantity maximum not represented in its inputs. If any leg is unexecutable, decline the entire basket; no replacement rank, unequal top-up or redistribution. Lot rounding creates disclosed small entry-notional imbalance. Retain fixed quantities between transitions; neither profits nor marks trigger minute notional rebalancing. Actual fills can differ in price/timing and temporarily break dollar balance.

Assumed taker fee **5bps** and uniform adverse slippage **8bps for all eight instruments**; participation **5%** of prior completed native trade volume. Joint stress uses **10bps fees,16bps uniform slippage**, adds one minute activation delay and halves participation to **2.5%**. Engine slippage configuration is account-wide; no per-instrument execution extension or core edit is assumed. Uniform8/16bps is the existing conservative joint-account model used for cross-venue/residual studies, higher than previous segregated BTC/ETH assumptions, declared before outcomes rather than introduced as a result-dependent adjustment. Tick rounding remains adverse. Planned unnetted roundtrip diagnostic is `2×Σ_i planned_notional_i×(fee_i+slip_i)/10000` under each run's costs; no netting opposite legs or crediting future appreciation. Actual costs use actual fills, not this diagnostic. Current exchange metadata approximates historical lot/risk rules; minute trade execution is not historical BBO or queue evidence.

Actual sourced funding contributes `−signed_native_quantity×sourced_settlement_mark×actual_rate` only for inventory existing at that cash event. Funding callbacks occur before same-clock open fills according to the frozen Engine contract; a new position cannot earn an already settled payment. Retain asynchronously timed native settlements, source precision and an available sourced END settlement if inventory remains. Price/realized P&L, fees, funding and liquidation charges must reconcile separately; mark-to-market equity already includes funding cash.

## Basket transitions, failures and hard limits

Every entry or exit target is immutable. Submit one native GTC market order per changed leg, retain its object, and do not cancel/recreate it on each callback. Entry completion requires all four actual signed native quantities within `max(1e-9,lot×1e-6)`, not merely accepted orders or a reported dollar balance. Deadline is original submission+configured activation delay+five minutes. Capture intended/actual quantities, all fills, unmatched dollar exposure and duration.

Before replacing a basket, cancel outstanding entry intents and close all actual inventory with reduce-only GTC orders. No atomic flip or intermediate new exposure is permitted. Scheduled Sunday 00:05 rolling closes the existing basket before replacing it, regardless of whether its ranks changed. A hard holding-age fallback expires **seven elapsed days from actual four-leg completion**, never extended by changing ranks, pending orders or callbacks. The scheduled weekly roll normally acts before that fallback; the fallback remains active at every minute if the scheduled callback is unavailable. No new entry at or after `phase_stop−7days`. Exit orders receive the same immutable activation+five-minute deadline. Persist executable cleanup rather than declare terminal inventory flat; no fabricated endpoint fills. Subminimum dust is marked, reported and blocks entries.

On rejection/cancellation of an unmet leg, overshoot, entry/exit deadline failure or actual liquidation, abort the entire basket, cancel tracked opening orders and persist reduce-only cleanup. Persistent zero-capacity legs cannot be hidden by closing profitable legs; the account remains blocked until truly flat. After failed/risk cleanup, require **24 hours after actual flatness** before new entries, then wait for the next weekly selection clock. Every actual liquidation latches failure and cooldown even when the core already leaves all positions flat.

At every completed minute, account equity<=80% initial capital, nonpositive free margin, actual gross>.5×current equity, or maintenance plus configured taker/liquidation-close reserve>=80% current equity triggers whole-account abort. Core margin/liquidation enforcement remains active and may precede an executable exit. These are requests for future cleanup, not guaranteed loss bounds. Disclose gross/net/beta-unhedged exposure, cross margin, maintenance, available cash, all actual liquidation events and residual risk.

A normal scheduled roll has **no 24h cooldown**. Freeze the selected identities, orientation, yields and rank order at Sunday 00:05; that proposal expires at **00:20 UTC** (a fixed 15-minute engineering budget). The old basket receives a single close-to-flat transaction whose deadline is 00:05+configured activation delay+five minutes. No opening plan is allowed until all actual positions are flat and tracked old orders are resolved. On its deadline failure, cancel that transaction, persist cleanup, start the failure cooldown only after genuine flatness and abandon the proposal.

After timely actual flatness, use **then-known account equity, available cash and native completed prices** to create the new equal-budget quantities, retaining the frozen Sunday identities/ranks. This is a fresh admission, not restoration of old quantities or a new rank lookup. Freeze all four GTC targets only after aggregate lot/minimum/margin/gross admission. New entry submission must be strictly before 00:20; its immutable deadline is its own submission+configured delay+five minutes, which may extend beyond proposal expiry. Old close and new entry occur on separate callbacks; flatness does not make them atomic. Expired proposals are skipped without Monday catch-up. Any later entry failure invokes whole-basket cleanup and the 24h failure cooldown. A normal daily signal exit likewise requires actual flatness; the next permissible entry is a future Sunday proposal. Weekly selection and all cleanup clocks continue in the no-entry final seven days, so final inventory can receive genuine exits without fabricated endpoint prices.

At the known completed-minute callback **phase_stop−10minutes**, latch terminal mode before any new proposal or entry processing. Cancel outstanding opening intents/proposals and submit reduce-only GTC orders sized from each **actual remaining** native position. Already live valid reduce-only exit/cleanup orders are retained rather than canceled/recreated; their immutable original deadlines remain binding. Newly submitted terminal exits have deadline original terminal submission+configured activation delay+five minutes (base stop−5minutes; delayed stress stop−4minutes), leaving time for an observed failure and persistent cleanup before the endpoint. Repeated callbacks do not resubmit or extend terminal orders. The final eligible trade-bar open is `phase_stop−1minute`; activation at/after phase_stop cannot receive a fabricated fill. An END funding event may exist without another strategy callback, so terminal action must not depend on such a callback. At phase_stop retain actual sourced funding cash and final marks, report any unresolved orders, dust or inventory and fail gate15. Indicative exit reserves remain disclosed even for such failing residual phases; reserve arithmetic is not a substitute for actual flatness. This stricter terminal intent is frozen for this weekly strategy, not retroactively imposed on earlier marked-open protocols.

The coordinator has one global state: `IDLE`, `ENTERING`, `HOLDING`, `EXITING`, `ABORTING`, `COOLDOWN` or `BLOCKED`. A Sunday proposal is a separate immutable object and never overrides a transaction in progress. `ENTERING` becomes `HOLDING` only after actual four-leg completion; a deadline/failure goes to `ABORTING`. `EXITING` reaches `IDLE` only after actual flatness and order resolution, then a still-live scheduled proposal may be admitted on a later callback. An abort is latched once: repeated risk callbacks do not recreate cleanup orders, move its deadlines or restart cooldown. `ABORTING` retains its reduce-only GTC cleanup orders across callbacks; passing an immutable cleanup deadline records failure and keeps the account blocked while still-executable orders remain live. Rejected/cancelled cleanup or unexecutable dust becomes `BLOCKED`, with native residuals marked and no automatic retry or new entry. Truly flat completed abort cleanup reaches `COOLDOWN`, timed from its actual flatness; liquidation while already flat must still reset any ghost basket and start cooldown. Terminal callbacks only report these actual states and orders; they do not restore targets, erase incomplete intents or force fills.
