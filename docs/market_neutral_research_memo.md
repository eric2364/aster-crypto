# Relative-value crypto research assessment — no new numerical experiment

> This memo is a pre-correction assessment based on preserved original-engine results. The [audited corrected comparison](margin_correction_results/report.html) supersedes its derivative performance figures and still reports 0/24 complete hurdle passes. No new market-neutral strategy was tested by this memo.

2026-10-06. This memo reads the completed evidence and primary research only. No new fitting, signal calculation, threshold selection or Engine run was performed, and no frozen package/model/result was changed.

**Assessment:** a jointly executed factor-hedged residual-convergence portfolio is a plausible additional research family. It tests relative mispricing after removing shared exposure, which the prior outright mean-reversion and daily momentum-rank portfolios did not directly test. It can improve the diversity of the research, but neither the existing data nor the papers justify expecting four or five robust successful strategies. Pair selection, cointegration, PCA and factor-regression implementations are related statistical-arbitrage variants, not independent economic edges.

## What the completed evidence says

The original thirteen policies and the six signed hypotheses passed zero of their declared hurdles; the five external-factor candidates also passed zero. The external funding-crowding policy is a useful nuance: pooled validation +0.3443%, reused evaluation +0.05284%, joint stress +0.03301%, but only one profitable evaluation asset and six fills; it failed the predefined breadth requirement. This does not justify relaxing that gate or converting one concentrated result into a diversified success. The external premium fade had zero evaluation fills, not evidence of profitable convergence. Taker-flow drift lost 29.633% in evaluation and 47.792% under joint stress. Those results come directly from [the unchanged external summary](external_factor_results/summary.json).

The signed cross-sectional momentum basket lost 3.025% in evaluation and 3.767% under stress despite positive validation; its long/short initial notional was balanced, but it explicitly did not estimate factor neutrality. Outright signed mean reversion lost 7.499% in evaluation. See [the unchanged directional summary](minute_directional_results/summary.json). These failures leave a qualitative rationale for studying a different object—the hedged residual—but do not identify a profitable reversal threshold, pair, hedge ratio, holding period or model direction. This memo specifies none of those from the known outcomes.

## Primary evidence and transfer limits

- [Gatev, Goetzmann and Rouwenhorst, Pairs Trading](https://www.nber.org/papers/w7032) studies distance-matched US equities and distinguishes the pairs effect from generic mean reversion using randomized pairs. It supports testing relative-value convergence, not assuming cointegration among unrelated tokens. Its historical equity universe, institutional setting and transaction costs do not establish a contemporary Binance edge.
- [Avellaneda and Lee, Statistical Arbitrage in the US Equities Market, author-hosted manuscript](https://math.nyu.edu/inmemoriam/avellaneda/AvellanedaLeeStatArb20090616.pdf) models factor residuals and trades their mean reversion. PCA and ETF regressions are alternative ways to remove common exposure. The authors also document performance degradation and liquidity-crisis losses: hedging does not eliminate crowding or execution risk. With eight tokens, a small interpretable factor model is preferable to copying their broad-equity PCA architecture and claiming equivalent diversification.
- [Tadi and Kortchmeski, Dynamic Cointegration-Based Pairs Trading in Cryptocurrency](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.10662) is directly relevant crypto research. It uses cointegration tests and mean-reversion estimates; its execution study includes bid/ask quotes and available quoted size. Our minute OHLCV participation approximation lacks that information, so an experiment could test an adaptation, not reproduce its execution evidence. Dynamic optimal lookbacks and multiple specification choices would add tuning degrees of freedom.
- [Liu, Tsyvinski and Wu, Common Risk Factors in Cryptocurrency](https://www.nber.org/papers/w25882) motivates separating broad crypto market and momentum exposure from idiosyncratic returns. The fixed eight-token dataset lacks the historical comprehensive universe and capitalization data needed to replicate its size factor. BTC/ETH return proxies must be called proxies, not a reproduced cryptocurrency factor model.
- [Makarov and Schoar, Trading and Arbitrage in Cryptocurrency Markets, author manuscript at MIT](https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/7f91bfb5-ba77-4d0e-9c79-ec75e104e6cc) documents exchange segmentation and cross-exchange deviations. All current target prices come from one venue; those data cannot substantiate cross-exchange, cross-border or transfer arbitrage. Those mechanisms are economically distinct but currently infeasible without additional allowed data and transfer/execution assumptions.

## Feasible research choices, before any economic definition

| Approach | New research object | What must be resolved before running |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed economic-pair convergence | Relative spread between two related native perpetuals, with both legs actually executed | Related narratives are not a redemption/valuation identity. BTC/ETH or DOGE/PEPE can be candidate peer relationships, but neither has a binding common fundamental price. Pair identities and exclusion tests must be decided before numerical outputs; do not search all 28 combinations and advertise the best historical pair. |
| BTC/ETH-factor residual convergence | Each altcoin return stripped of a small set of tradable broad-market proxy exposures, with the corresponding proxy hedge legs | Return regression removes estimated linear exposure; it does not prove that the accumulated residual is stationary or predictable. Need predeclared training-only estimation, stability/mean-reversion diagnostics, deterministic no-trade handling and an independent structural-break check. |
| Small-universe PCA residuals | Common latent exposure removed from the eight synchronized instruments | Feasible computationally but fragile economically: eight variables, overlapping return histories, changing factor directions and model choice introduce instability. Not recommended as another independent family alongside regression residuals. |

The first two are reasonable alternative implementations of **one residual-relative-value family**. My preference for the next research decision is an interpretable BTC/ETH-proxy residual portfolio, with a fixed economic-pair diagnostic as a comparator rather than a second claimed edge. This preference is qualitative; no fitted coefficients or outcomes were inspected. Root coordination is required before specifying the economic packet, thresholds or running a new cohort.

For clarity, dollar neutrality and estimated beta neutrality differ. If an asset's return loading on a reference is β, an illustrative first-order hedge uses asset dollar exposure a and reference exposure −βa. Native quantities are a/P_asset and −βa/P_reference. Gross notional is |a|(1+|β|), and net dollar notional is a(1−β); it is generally not zero. Equal-dollar long/short legs leave estimated beta exposure unless β=1. A log-level cointegration coefficient and a return-regression beta are distinct estimands and must not be silently substituted. These are accounting/model relationships, not selected hedge parameters. Native 1000PEPE prices remain prices per bundle; percentage returns are unit invariant and Engine quantities remain native.

## Practical requirements and realistic evidence boundary

A paired strategy must run all legs in one actual portfolio simulation, rather than subtracting independently profitable equity curves. Each leg pays real assumed fees/slippage and actual funding; shared symbols must net correctly. Independent partial fills, delayed orders, native lot constraints and isolated collateral can temporarily destroy the intended hedge. Record residual exposures and liquidation events; use a predeclared pending-order/failed-leg policy. Market-price co-movement cannot collateralize an isolated losing leg automatically. Report realized gross/net notional and estimated factor exposure over time, not an unconditional “market-neutral” label.

Use slow decisions and the existing minute execution/mark clock to avoid pretending the dataset observes high-frequency convergence or queues. Training-only stability tests must account for dependence: half a year of minute bars is not hundreds of thousands of independent equilibrium observations. Searching pairs, regression factors, stationarity tests and lookbacks creates additional trials even when the final display contains only one curve. A deterministic no-trade outcome when no relationship is admissible is a valid research failure, not a reason to widen criteria.

Preserve the original fees, joint stress, genuine marked terminal inventory, reserve diagnostic, source hashes and ledger identities. Basket success must be evaluated at portfolio level: losing hedge legs may be necessary, so five profitable individual hedge legs is not a coherent criterion. Any revised breadth requirement would need economic justification **before** results, together with explicit reporting of every leg and candidate; it cannot retroactively rescue prior failed cohorts.

The same July–October evaluation has now been repeatedly inspected. A new pass on it would be an exploratory historical finding requiring future independent evaluation, not proof of robustness or non-overfitting. The fixed surviving eight-token universe and one year cannot establish persistence across many crypto regimes. A single new relative-value family can meaningfully improve the experiment's economic coverage. It cannot credibly supply four or five independent successful examples merely by renaming pairs, factors, scales or models.
