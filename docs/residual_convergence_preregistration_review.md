# Independent preregistration review: factor residual convergence

2026-10-06. Design review only: no historical fitting, signal generation or new numerical outcomes. Existing core and frozen models are unchanged. The one permissible new economic family is an altcoin portfolio hedged with tradable BTC/ETH perpetual return proxies; it cannot supply four independent edges through different assets or parameter names.

## Estimator and state definition

Do not equate return-beta estimation with log-level cointegration. A return regression estimates short-horizon exposures; a log-level regression estimates a putative equilibrium relation. High return correlation or a finite fitted OU half-life does not establish an equilibrium.

In-sample OLS residuals with an intercept sum to zero. Their cumulative sum therefore returns to zero at the fitted sample endpoint. Fitting OU dynamics to that same path risks treating a regression constraint as economic convergence. Avellaneda–Lee explicitly identifies the endpoint artifact and discusses separate regression/process estimation windows. Their equity results are motivation, not evidence of Binance profitability. [Author manuscript, appendix](https://math.nyu.edu/inmemoriam/avellaneda/AvellanedaLeeStatArb20090616.pdf).

For this proposed return-beta family, freeze one definition before fitting: hourly completed log returns; intercept and BTC/ETH coefficients fitted on the first training subperiod; a cumulative residual starting at a declared origin on a later, disjoint training diagnostic subperiod. Preserve the fitted coefficients, origin and cumulative state through later phases. Fit OU/AR(1) parameters only on the diagnostic block. No retrospective re-estimation of prior increments, rolling endpoint pinning, phase rebasing to a new mean, or artificial reset after a loss. A gap invalidates new signals; it does not remove existing exposure or its economic loss.

A concrete conservative allocation is the first 90 days for beta/intercept estimation and the remaining first-six-month observations for process diagnostics, leaving the next three months for validation and final three for reused evaluation. Keeping those coefficients fixed thereafter is easier to audit than adaptive refitting. If a different causal refit schedule is desired, freeze the schedule, state continuity, admissibility and coefficient-change hedge policy before any fit; it is an additional specification.

If log-level cointegration is chosen instead, explicitly change the estimator and disclosure. Residual-based cointegration tests require the appropriate generated-residual critical values, deterministic terms and regressor count, not a generic ADF threshold applied after choosing a favorable spread. [MacKinnon, primary working paper](https://qed.econ.queensu.ca/pub/faculty/mackinnon/working-papers/qed_wp_1227.pdf). Do not test both estimators and present only the winner as a single prespecified trial.

## Training-only admissibility and no-trade behavior

Declare the six altcoin identities in advance: SOL, XRP, LINK, ADA, DOGE and native 1000PEPE; BTC/ETH are hedge proxies. Include every excluded relationship and its reason in the output. Never rank the 28 possible pairs or select the best altcoin using validation/evaluation PnL.

Before fitting, choose exact numeric limits for sample size, missing data, factor-design conditioning, coefficient magnitude and uncertainty, coefficient stability across predefined training subblocks, residual variance, process stability and cost coverage. A stable positive AR coefficient below one is needed for the usual real-valued OU mapping; nonfinite, zero-variance, negative/oscillatory, unit-root-like or explosive fits must produce no trades under that mapping. State the sampling interval in the half-life formula: `half_life = -log(2) * interval / log(phi)`. Predefine admissible half-life relative to both the observed diagnostic span and maximum holding period, rather than clipping invalid estimates into profitability.

Use a predeclared dependence-aware uncertainty procedure for diagnostic stability. A large number of minute observations is not the same number of independent equilibrium excursions. Separate admissibility diagnostics from trading outcomes: no-trade is a valid failure, and an eligibility gate must not be widened after seeing zero admitted assets. Six asset diagnostics are multiple tests; account for them if reporting inferential significance. Purely descriptive stability limits should be labeled descriptive.

After the training cutoff, validation/evaluation may disable risk through a predefined causal break rule, but may not select new factors, thresholds, coefficients or eligible identities. A break kill-switch should not automatically reactivate unless its prospective rule is already frozen. Full-year fitted means, variances, beta values, asset ranks and half-lives are prohibited inputs to earlier decisions.

## Executable cost and hedge rules

For altcoin dollar target `a`, initial proxy hedge dollars are `-beta_BTC*a` and `-beta_ETH*a`. Native quantities are each signed dollar target divided by its own native traded price, subject to native lots and minima. Gross target notional is `abs(a)*(1+abs(beta_BTC)+abs(beta_ETH))`; beta neutrality does not imply dollar neutrality. Return calculations for 1000PEPE are unit invariant, but order quantity must remain in bundles. A log-level coefficient must not silently replace a return beta.

Use one actual multi-instrument account or explicitly isolated three-leg sleeves with their real capital allocations. Shared BTC/ETH exposure must be netted only within the declared account structure. Subtracting separately simulated equity curves does not implement a hedge. A fixed native-quantity hedge is initially beta balanced but drifts with relative prices; record realized factor exposures and approximation error. Any rebalancing rule creates real turnover and fees and must be frozen.

Entry requires a prospectively calculated convergence budget exceeding all intended legs' round-trip fees/slippage under the declared conservative cost rule, plus an explicitly defined funding/risk allowance. Use current executable assumptions and information available at the decision; no future realized funding or favorable future exit price may enter the budget. With a drift-adjusted cumulative residual, expected hedged PnL includes the fitted intercept contribution: subtracting drift from the signal does not remove drift from the tradable portfolio. An adverse drift can overwhelm residual convergence. Report the prediction as an approximation, never as guaranteed proceeds.

Retain existing taker fees, adverse slippage, actual marks/funding and joint stress (double fees/slippage, additional bar delay and half participation). Do not reduce cost assumptions to admit a relationship. Fix entry/exit thresholds, maximum hold, stop/break behavior, review clock, cooldown, allocation and any neighboring diagnostics before fitting. Prefer one primary specification over a performance-ranked scale grid. Neighbors are sensitivity tests, not an invitation to choose a surviving evaluation variant.

Engine callbacks see the complete simultaneous close slice before subsequent opens. Build decisions from `on_bars` or a precomputed complete-grid feature snapshot; `on_bar` ordering across instruments must not expose only some legs' new closes. A strategy still must obey configured latency/delay, independent volume constraints, native lot rounding and isolated margin. Declare failed-leg timeout, pending-order cancellation and bounded cleanup before running. A partly filled hedge cannot disappear from exposure/PnL or receive an imaginary close. Terminal positions remain marked with an indicative exit reserve unless actual closing orders fill.

## Minimal independent tests before execution

1. Prefix and future-perturbation invariance for coefficients, admissibility, cumulative state and targets; changing validation/evaluation leaves every training artifact unchanged.
2. Completed-hour boundaries, exact UTC timestamps, same-clock asset order permutation, missing/duplicate/native-ID rejection and gap handling. No backward filling of a missing factor close.
3. Synthetic known-beta returns recover the declared relationship; multiplying all 1000PEPE prices by a constant leaves returns/signals unchanged while native quantities transform correctly. Constant/collinear factors fail deterministically.
4. Random-walk or drifting residual examples demonstrate that an in-sample zero-sum residual bridge is not accepted as independent evidence; invalid AR coefficients, zero variance and too-short diagnostic histories remain no-trade. Include a stable constructed process solely to test arithmetic and direction.
5. Synthetic positive/negative intercept examples prove that the cost gate includes actual hedged drift. Increasing any leg's fee/slippage cannot turn a previously rejected opportunity into an accepted one.
6. A three-leg Engine handcase checks actual quantities, proportional reserve, fees, funding signs, shared-factor netting and cash conservation. Force one leg's volume to zero and verify bounded pending/cleanup behavior, real residual exposure and stress delay at the final eligible open.
7. Frozen artifact tampering, changed training cutoff, asset exclusion or specification identity invalidates reuse. All trial identities, inactive cases, liquidations and terminal inventories remain in the report.

No implementation is available yet for these source-level tests. Tests should exercise public signal/estimation and portfolio interfaces once they exist; avoid assertions that merely mirror implementation lines.

## Review of the root's fixed packet proposal

The proposed dates are precise and defensible for this exploratory test: fit hourly return OLS on October 5–January 5; diagnose the resulting out-of-sample cumulative residual and fit OU on January 5–April 5; keep coefficients, cumulative origin and equilibrium fixed through April–October. This disjoint fit/diagnostic design addresses the specific endpoint constraint. Quarter account resets must reset cash/inventory only, never residual state or equilibrium. Source cutoffs should use completed bar ends; coefficients and calibration must carry availability timestamps no earlier than their final used observation.

The proposed single specification has entry `abs(z)>=2`, exit `abs(z)<=0.5`, stop `abs(z)>=4`, maximum hold 48 hours and a 24-hour cooldown after a stop. An extreme deviation at or beyond the stop cannot initiate a new position, and exiting/entry rules must have explicit precedence. No halved/doubled parameter selection is needed. Fix the decision clock and whether a cooldown/stop state continues across independently initialized quarter accounts. Signals using minute observations against an hourly calibrated equilibrium are a modeling choice, not additional independent hourly information.

The proposed admissibility limits are half-life 1–24 hours, conditional ADF `p<=0.01`, fixed lag 24, design condition at most 100 and `abs(beta_BTC)+abs(beta_ETH)<=4`. Require finite values, `0<phi<1` and positive innovation/equilibrium variance before mapping half-life. Compute design conditioning on centered/standardized factor columns: a raw intercept beside tiny return columns is unit sensitive. Freeze the ADF deterministic term (`regression='c'`) and disable automatic lag search (`autolag=None`). Its p-value is a conditional diagnostic of this constructed residual; six nominal one-percent tests do not certify familywise five-percent stationarity. Use no joint significance claim, or separately preregister a corrected diagnostic without tuning to results. Beta/OU uncertainty and structural stability remain reported limitations even when these eligibility thresholds pass.

The proposal's cost gate is conservative if defined consistently: projected 48-hour OU displacement recovery minus `abs(hourly_alpha)*48` must exceed twice the modeled round-trip cost, with all three leg weights included. Use the finite-horizon recovery `abs(x-mean)*(1-phi**48)`, not the full displacement or sigma interpreted as dollars. For small log displacements, this is an approximate return on altcoin notional; compare costs in the same units and expose that approximation. Prefer additionally reporting the implied gross-normalized budget. The actual portfolio must still pay funding and costs irrespective of a positive predicted budget.

Uniform baseline slippage 8 basis points and taker fee 5 per fill imply 26 basis points round-trip cost per gross initial notional; stress slippage 16 and fee 10 imply 52. Twice baseline round-trip cost therefore matches the modeled stress round-trip hurdle before funding, quantity drift, delayed/partial fills and cleanup. These are assumptions, not measured spreads. Actual forward funding is used only in account settlement, never in the prospective entry test. Define a funding allowance or explicitly disclose that this cost gate excludes unpredictable future funding.

The proposed joint gross limit is 25 percent of each $100,000 account, split into six fixed altcoin relationship allocations. Inadmissible or inactive allocations stay cash; do not increase the remaining relationships' budgets. Native lot rounding and shared BTC/ETH netting can change achieved hedge ratios; record target and actual exposure. The stress packet preserves doubled fees/slippage, one additional execution bar and participation 2.5 percent. Independent quarter wallets are a new prospective accounting definition for this family; clearly state their dates, genuine end inventory and monthly success calculations without changing earlier cohort gates.

**Design readiness:** acceptable as one fixed exploratory hypothesis once these units, clocks, precedence, conditioning and account-state contracts are explicit and synthetic tests pass. No data fit or outcome has been reviewed here. Zero admissible assets remains an acceptable result; a new historical pass would still require later prospective validation.
