# Evidence review: flow exhaustion and conditional reversal

Reviewed 6 October 2026. This is a source and mechanism review, with no historical outcome scans, new fits, code changes or strategy runs. The fixed research window remains **2025-10-05 00:00 UTC to 2026-10-05 00:00 UTC**; older academic samples are evidence, not additional backtest data.

**Assessment:** volatility/liquidity-conditioned reversal is a defensible research hypothesis distinct from unconditional flow continuation. The primary evidence does not establish a net-cost, multi-hour exhaustion strategy on our eight native Binance perpetuals. It supports one related falsification candidate, not four or five independent profitable mechanisms. I would not yet freeze a trading protocol with invented exhaustion thresholds or claim that longer holding periods solve costs.

There is a reproducibility warning in WP413's inspected version: §2.2/§3.2 describe **30bp long /40bp short**, while the Table5 caption states **20bp/30bp**, applied on daily rebalancing. I would not treat its exact published net-cost number as an authoritative execution benchmark without resolving that discrepancy. Its own text says the exercise is not a real-time trading recipe. This is a limitation of transferring the paper, not a claim that its conditional-reversal evidence is invalid.

The 2026 preprint's sample and later reported holdout overlap our already examined year. Its external publication is useful mechanism evidence, but does not restore a fresh holdout here. Statistical sign accuracy, gross expectation and realistically capturable net P&L remain separate questions.

## Distinction from the tested families

Our frozen `taker_flow_drift` rule follows an aggregate imbalance over roughly an hour, without price confirmation, and exits on weakening/opposition or a time limit. A conditional reversal rule would instead require a large **observed price displacement aligned with aggressive flow**, followed by **subsequently observed loss of that pressure**, before taking the opposite direction. That changes both the hypothesis and entry event; it is more than negating the old signal.

It nevertheless shares price reversal, aggregate flow and liquidity proxies with prior research. Variations in shock duration, volatility bands, confirmation windows, DOGE/PEPE subsets or exits are variants of one hypothesis. The old continuation loss is not evidence that its inverse has positive expectation. A future conditional-flow family should be counted once as the next exploratory comparison, with any variants disclosed rather than counted as additional successes.

The precise proposed mechanism—multi-hour forced-flow exhaustion followed by inventory recovery—has **not** been identified by the papers above. Lower activity in a cross-sectional daily sort is not the same as a temporary high-volume selloff in one coin. Greater volatility increases potential displacement and adverse selection simultaneously. Confirmation may remove the pressure event or arrive after its small rebound has already occurred. These are reasons to test, not explanations of known P&L.

## Feasibility and execution limits on existing sources

The current normalized flow extractor retains `total_volume`, `taker_buy_volume`, `trade_count`, native identity and explicit `available_at`. It reads actual original USD-M kline fields and verifies their volumes against execution bars. The declared availability is minute start plus two minutes: completed minute plus one extra publication minute. This is an assumption, not an historical receipt. Inspecting those source definitions did not require reading real feature intensities or outcomes. [Existing extractor](../examples/external_factors/extract_flow.py), [frozen drift protocol](../examples/external_factors/proposed_protocol.md).

For any complete window, aggressive imbalance can be measured as `(2*sum(buy)-sum(total))/sum(total)`; nonpositive total makes it undefined. Realized volatility, return displacement, relative traded activity and a return-per-dollar-volume proxy are computable causally. Such proxies do not reveal spread, displayed depth, inventory, informed flow, liquidation origin or queue position. Binance documents USD-M kline total/taker-buy **base** volume and separate quote volume, along with archive checksums and possible later archive corrections. [Official archive definitions](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data#klines-1).

DOGE quantities remain DOGE; the perpetual labelled PEPEUSDT in our universe is natively `1000PEPEUSDT`. Its price and base quantity remain bundled units. Ratios of buys to total and log returns are scale-invariant, whereas native size, notional, volume caps and lot/minimum constraints are not interchangeable with spot PEPE. A dollar-volume proxy formed from price times total base volume is only an approximation to true quote turnover; the existing normalized flow stream does not retain quote turnover. It must be named accordingly, or authentic quote turnover must be re-extracted into a new separately sealed input without changing prior datasets.

A minute observation clock can support multi-hour holdings, but entries and confirmation must use only jointly available completed observations. All rolling baselines must lie inside the fixed year; insufficient initial history stays flat, gaps reset windows, and event groups cannot be split across selection/evaluation boundaries. No August episode, venue anomaly, coin or volatility threshold may be selected because its previously observed return was attractive.

Existing perpetual execution can model signed positions, actual funding, partial taker fills, delays and liquidation. Under the recent fixed prospective assumptions, near-equal entry/exit notionals cost approximately **26bp round-trip** at baseline (two times5bp fees plus8bp adverse slippage), and **52bp** under joint stress (two times10bp plus16bp). Funding and unequal exit notionals alter the actual cash result. These are our assumptions, not the papers' costs. A forecast of half a shock's magnitude is not justified expected recovery. Prior-volume participation is also an approximation, especially precisely when liquidity is stressed. Maker fill/rebate shortcuts remain unsupported by these candles.

## Next decision

The evidence justifies a written **conditional temporary-pressure hypothesis**, not a calibrated trading rule. I do not propose fixed numerical trading thresholds here: neither the multi-hour exhaustion horizon nor a net recovery/cost budget is established by the inspected sources. Adding thresholds now would make engineering choices appear paper-derived.

If the team chooses one further falsification study, freeze its one family and all engineering thresholds before inspecting any new outcomes; retain realistic fees/slippage, actual funding, native minimums, participation, post-fill accounting, broad asset requirements and all-phase liquidation checks. Require source-causal synthetic tests and separately disclose the event count, independent shock clusters, trade count and concentration, including DOGE and native PEPE. A no-trade or failed result is valid. A joint time-block, familywise comparison must include the previous 26 families; the reused year cannot establish absence of overfitting.

There is no evidence here that continued searches on this same year will produce four or five robust winners. A more defensible route to that goal is a source-supported mechanism with credible cost scale, followed by genuinely prospective paper trading on new observations. Neither an academic gross effect nor a successful synthetic execution test would establish our own strategy's profitability.
