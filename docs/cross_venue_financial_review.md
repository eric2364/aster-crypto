# Independent financial-verifier review

This review concerns `audit/verify_cross_venue_outputs.py`, not a change to the frozen research packet. Synthetic adversaries and source inspection checked venue-local cash, current-mark cross margin, partial-close fee/funding allocation, same-clock marks/settlements/open fills, immutable order activation and cancellation, source prices/prior-volume capacity, terminal inventory, exit reserves, all descriptive metrics and the26-comparison circular bootstrap.

The monetary replay reconstructs recorded linear perpetual cash correctly: opening principal is reserved rather than spent; realized close P&L and fees affect only that venue; funding uses actual positions and sourced settlement valuations; matched gains cannot rescue another wallet's liquidation. Current-mark margin is distinct from the entry allocation field. The reviewed clock order matches completed marks, pre-funding liquidation, all settlement marks, funding cash, strategy callbacks and next open execution. Reserve costs are analysis only, not another cash debit. Full-resolution marked drawdown and endpoint-adjusted daily bootstrap formulas preserve the declared gates.

A material scope limit remains: financially consistent records do not prove generic strategy intent reproduction. The verifier does not independently rebuild all feature-based entry quantities/orientations, the cost-admission rule, every missed order, horizon/cooldown or the entire failed-leg state machine. Its matched-entry check ties reported targets to actual quantities and diagnostics, but those diagnostics are not an independent decision oracle. Its executable recorded-fill checks do not establish completeness of entirely absent pending-leg fills.

The independent reviewer constructed a coherent partial/failed-leg export with absent Bybit fills, then supplied a source with available Bybit capacity. Recorded-cash replay still accepted the absent leg because there was no recorded fill to test. This is retained as a synthetic scope regression, not a claim about the actual cohort. The financial certificate therefore explicitly does **not** certify generic strategy-policy reconstruction. The financial publisher must avoid that claim for a trading cohort.

For this completed zero-activity cohort, a separate independent proof closes the omission question narrowly: `audit/verify_cross_venue_no_admission.py` checks every eligible hourly clock and all eight pairs in all seven fixed phases against the immutable, independently reconstructed feature frame. It independently forms the exact rational common native grid, quantity caps, both opening minima, orientation and the unnetted joint-stress forecast/cost rule. It conservatively includes the float-ideal floor path and the coordinator's notional epsilon. Additionally, because quantity cancels, it checks a unit-notional forecast/cost upper bound before native minima or margin rejection. Any potential admission, including a floating comparator boundary, fails the zero-cohort certificate rather than being explained away.

The no-admission proof also requires genuinely zero orders/events/fills/funding cash/trades/open inventory/matched entries, both wallets fixed at50,000 USDT and pooled equity100,000, every sealed phase's25 output hashes and the same immutable source/feature/summary packet. It is deliberately **cohort-specific**, not a generic execution or policy certificate. A future trading cohort needs independent intent/state-machine reproduction before that stronger claim.

No economic threshold, source dataset, frozen coordinator, Engine code or historical outcome was changed during this review. Earlier reports remain preserved.
