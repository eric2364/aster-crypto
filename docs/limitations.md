# Verified scope and limitations

This repository is a research MVP with independently tested accounting, not a validated production exchange emulator. Synthetic fixtures remain explicitly labelled. Real one-year Binance demonstrations use downloaded archived prices with source manifests; their model returns do not demonstrate tradable profitability.

Implemented: cash-only long spot; one-way linear perpetual longs/shorts in base quantity; per-exchange quote wallets; a single settlement currency; globally selected isolated or cross margin; maker/taker fees including signed rebates; explicit funding settlements; flat maintenance with closing-cost reserve; mark-triggered full liquidation; market/limit/stop-market/stop-limit; post-only/reduce-only and GTC/IOC/FOK; participation-limited partial fills; fixed/spread/volatility/volume slippage; bar-quantized order latency; closed-trade and open-position accounting; array research; grid search, independent-window walk-forward, cost/liquidity/delay robustness, daily/monthly/rolling/regime analytics and reports.

OHLC execution lacks bid/ask paths, queue priority, hidden liquidity, intrabar event ordering, endogenous market impact and microsecond matching. Opening capacity uses the last completed interval's volume as a causal liquidity proxy. Realized current-bar volume caps end-of-bar maker fills after subtracting opening consumption; actual capacity can differ from the proxy, so a causal opening fill can exceed subsequently observed volume. No promise of exact physical volume conservation across that unknowable opening estimate is made. Conservative limits require a full resting interval and strict penetration beyond a tick; touch is deliberately more optimistic. `volume_aware` currently shares conservative price eligibility and deterministic participation caps, not calibrated fill probabilities. All fill profiles cap volume. GTC market remainders retry later openings; use IOC for one-shot market sweeps. Cancellation latency is zero.

New intrabar stop triggers execute a child at a later open; this may miss a real intrabar stop fill. Same-time completed limit fills run before funding, while strategy-generated new opening fills run after funding. Mark observations risk-check before funding, then all simultaneous funding flows are applied before the next risk check. This is a declared deterministic tie policy. Same-bar historical-close execution is intentionally not offered; next-open or delayed openings are causal supported choices. Positive millisecond latency is rounded forward to the next available opening.

Derivative risk sees open/end marks and funding-event marks. If missing, traded prices are proxies with a warning. No intrabar mark path is inferred. Fee schedules, increments, leverage and maintenance are dated configuration inputs, not automatically fetched or reconstructed venue tiers. Binance/Bybit/OKX names select offline adapters; they do not imply exact exchange liquidation or funding-debit behavior. Isolated funding debits position allocation; opening fees draw on free wallet; partial close retains realized P&L less close fee in remaining allocation. Pending margin is not reserved: orders are admitted sequentially at fill time. Forced liquidation ignores bar-volume limits so insolvent positions are closed and deficits remain visible.

Unsupported contracts reject: inverse, quanto, non-unit multipliers and dated futures/options. Also unimplemented: hedge-mode dual legs on the same ID, mixed margin modes, cross-currency collateral/haircuts, automatic wallet transfers, withdrawal/deposit delay, partial liquidation, historical risk tiers, insurance/ADL/bankruptcy transfers, live trading, historical download from venues other than Binance, trailing/OCO orders and L2 replay. Portfolio aggregation assumes one quote currency. Equal-notional basket targets drift with prices and independent venue capital; baskets are not guaranteed beta-neutral.

Trade rows represent average-cost closed lots, not necessarily one exchange order or one fully closed position episode. Holding duration starts at the first entry of that average-cost position. Costs/funding allocate pro rata on reduction. Open lots appear separately and closed-symbol attribution does not equal total equity change until open-lot P&L is included. Ratios use 365-day UTC sampling and zero risk-free hurdle; very short samples yield unstable annualization. Undefined ratios are null. Gross traded P&L includes slippage; diagnostic slippage is not a second debit. Regime labels are lagged descriptive heuristics. Walk-forward resets capital and indicator warmup in each holdout; fold returns are not a stitched self-financing portfolio.

Materialized schedules/history dominate event-memory consumption. Full 20-symbol × five-year / 100-symbol × two-year event benchmarks were not run. Large vector indicator benchmarks are separate and do not include matching/accounting. Funding coverage, historical token universes and exchange outages require provenance from the researcher.

Highest-value next work: streaming Arrow event merge and disk ledger writers; externally sourced historical funding/listings/mark data with coverage manifests; venue margin-tier and funding-debit policies; causal quote/trade/L2 queue simulation; finer mark risk clock; stitched walk-forward with explicit warmup; asynchronous transfers and contract-specific valuation.
