# Proposed external-factor exploratory cohort — design only

Status: proposed economic packet with five candidates; awaiting independent audit and source freeze, written before running any model in this cohort. No external-factor signals, PnL, selection scores or evaluation results have been calculated here. The original nineteen policies/hypotheses and their source locks remain unchanged. Their historical outcomes are already known, so this additional research uses a reused evaluation period and cannot supply an untouched-test or non-overfit proof.

There are five proposed hypotheses, not five demonstrated independent edges. Two introduce external macro information. The funding and basis hypotheses share a leveraged-demand/market-segmentation mechanism, overlap prior carry research, and must remain identified as related hypotheses. The fifth input is aggregate aggressive trade flow, rather than an additional price-only trend/reversal variant.

## Economic motivation and evidence limits

[IMF, Cryptic Connections (2022)](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/abstract/journals/065/2022/001/article-A001-en.xml) documents growing crypto/equity connectedness. This motivates testing external equity information, but connectedness is not evidence of an exploitable one-day QQQ lead. [Liu and Tsyvinski, Risks and Returns of Cryptocurrency](https://www.nber.org/papers/w24877) instead found limited exposure to many common macro factors in an earlier sample: that conflicting evidence is retained.

[Monetary Policy, Digital Assets, and DeFi Activity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.10252) studies unexpected US monetary-policy changes and digital-asset returns. Its result motivates the broad discount-rate channel; it does not establish an edge from actual CPI changes, which contain anticipated information. The CPI candidate below is our inference and uses no consensus, forecast or fabricated surprise. [An archived BLS release](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/cpi_09112025.htm) establishes the official release timestamp and distinguishes twelve-month unadjusted rates from seasonally adjusted monthly changes.

[BIS, Crypto carry](https://www.bis.org/publications/working-paper-1087-crypto-carry) links elevated futures carry to leveraged trend chasing, scarce arbitrage capital and crash risk. Its evidence concerns futures carry, primarily BTC/ETH; transferring the mechanism to settled perpetual funding or eight altcoin premiums is an explicit hypothesis, not a replication or proven causal direction. A perpetual has no dated expiry enforcing convergence. [Binance market-data documentation](https://developers.binance.com/en/docs/catalog/core-trading-derivatives-trading-usd-s-m-futures/api/rest-api/market-data) provides historical funding timestamps/rates and mark prices. Historical settlement rates are available observations, not forecasts of the next rate.

[Empirical Study of Market Impact Conditional on Order-Flow Imbalance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.08290) studies equity-market impact; it motivates a possible flow channel, not a demonstrated crypto continuation edge. [Binance public-data field definitions](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data) identify total and taker-buy base volume in archived klines. Our fifth rule uses aggregate aggressive trade volume, not level-two order-book OFI, queue depth or inferred liquidation prints.

## Shared data and availability contract

Target instruments are the existing eight native Binance linear perpetuals underlying BTC, ETH, DOGE, PEPE, SOL, XRP, LINK and ADA. Target observations stay within 2025-10-05 00:00 UTC through 2026-10-05 00:00 UTC, with the last tradable minute starting before the endpoint. Native `1000PEPEUSDT` prices and quantities remain unchanged. For a spot/perpetual comparison only, one native PEPE perpetual quantity unit corresponds to 1,000 PEPE spot units; divide native perpetual price by 1,000 to form a per-PEPE feature, never to price an Engine trade.

Each input has separate `observation_time`, `available_at`, original raw source and source hash. All joins are backward-only on `available_at <= decision_time`. No future interpolation, nearest join, backfill or date-only join is permitted.

- **QQQ:** unadjusted official/source daily close for US session date D; conservatively available at D+1 00:00 UTC, regardless of 20:00/21:00 UTC US close or early close. Use actual sessions only; never forward-fill prices into a rolling count of invented weekend sessions. Decision at 00:01 UTC. The most recent published close may be carried forward for at most 96 hours after its declared availability; then flatten until another observation appears. Split/dividend discontinuities are disclosed; retrospectively adjusted prices are not substituted. QQQ is a feature, not a tradable portfolio leg. The vendor `source_timestamp` denotes session opening time (09:30 New York), so it must not be mistaken for close availability. Historical original-delivery vintages are unknown; the next-UTC-day availability is an explicit conservative assumption, not a verified first-delivery timestamp.
- **CPI:** archived first-release headline and core CPI-U twelve-month percentage changes, with reference month, exact actual release timestamp converted using `America/New_York`, source URL and hash. Available one minute after the actual release. No subsequently revised series, reconstructed first releases from current series, forecast consensus, announcement-calendar assumptions or monthly values backdated to the reference month. Two releases must have consecutive reference months to calculate the acceleration. Missing/delayed releases yield no invented event. The current collection uses official BLS rendered-page responses where direct HTTP access returned 403; response hashes are hashes of those preserved rendered responses, not claimed original HTML bytes. The actual release header and as-reported values must be independently validated. All event observations must fall within the same fixed year. No pre-year baseline release may be imported; the first eligible acceleration needs two in-year releases with consecutive reference months.
- **Funding:** actual native settlement events become predictors one minute after their explicit actual receipt timestamp when supplied (receipt must be no earlier than settlement); absent a receipt timestamp, settlement plus one minute is the disclosed availability assumption. Cash settlement still occurs at its actual timestamp in Engine. Use the last three published rates only, require the newest to be no more than twelve hours old, and do not assume a fixed settlement interval. The next realized rate is never an input. Actual source events at the endpoint are retained if supplied.
- **Taker flow:** actual native one-minute archived total base volume and taker-buy base volume; `available_at = minute_start + two minutes`. Both volumes are in the same native quantity units. Reject negative/nonfinite volumes or buys exceeding total; zero aggregate rolling volume is undefined and flat. Gaps reset the complete rolling window.
- **Trade/spot features:** minute OHLCV and executable-price proxies become known at the completed minute, plus one extra minute for cross-feed features. Marks remain valuation/liquidation observations, not executable prices. Spot and perpetual premium calculations require exactly synchronized completed minutes; missing pairs reset warmup instead of interpolating. A mark/trade divergence must not be mislabeled spot/perpetual basis.

All durations below are based on UTC elapsed time or actual QQQ sessions as specified. Gaps reset minute-feature windows. NaNs, zero/nonfinite prices and missing availability produce flat signals. No history after each phase endpoint enters a signal.

## Five fixed candidate definitions

Let scale s be one of {0.8, 1.0, 1.2}. Thresholds, signs, decision schedules and feature sources are fixed; only explicitly stated durations/session counts vary. Round scaled minutes to the nearest whole minute and scaled session counts to the nearest integer, with ties upward. The entire duration set changes together; there is one common selected scale per hypothesis across all eight sleeves.

| Candidate | Fixed causal entry and sign | Exit and scaled component | Economic classification |
|---|---|---|---|
| `qqq_risk_transmission` | At 00:01 UTC, compute log QQQ return over round(5s) actual sessions and round(20s) sessions, using only published closes. Long when short return > log(1.01) and long return > 0; short when short return < -log(1.01) and long return < 0; otherwise flat. Identical macro direction applies to every crypto sleeve; no local-price confirmation is added. | Replace direction only at the next daily decision or stale-data flatten. Session lookbacks alone scale. Fixed quantity between decisions unless the direction changes. | External equity risk-appetite transmission. Correlated broad-market exposure, not eight independent signals. |
| `cpi_acceleration_event` | At first release + one minute, subtract previous consecutive first-release headline/core YoY percentage rates from the new respective rates. If both increase by at least 0.1 percentage point, short; if both decrease by at least 0.1 point, long; mixed/unchanged means flat. Compare published decimal values exactly (or with fixed tiny numerical tolerance). No price reaction or consensus surprise enters selection. | Hold at most round(1,440s) minutes after availability, then flat; a later actual event supersedes an earlier one. Only holding duration scales. | Inflation/discount-rate event drift conjecture. The change is already partly anticipated; only a handful of independent events may be observed. |
| `funding_crowding_fade` | Hourly at :01, mean the last three causally published settlement rates. Short if mean >= 0.0003; long if mean <= -0.0003; otherwise stay flat. This is unhedged directional crowding reversal, not delta-neutral carry or guaranteed funding income. | Flatten at hourly review when magnitude falls below 0.00015, or after round(1,440s) minutes since entry. Opposite entry requires the old position to exit first. Minimum one hour flat cooldown after every exit. Only maximum hold scales; last-three count and thresholds remain fixed. | Leveraged crowding/crash-risk mechanism; related to prior carry and the basis hypothesis. |
| `perpetual_premium_fade` | Hourly at :01, form median of log(perp trade close / matched-unit spot close) over the previous round(60s) synchronized minutes, excluding the extra publication minute. Short if median >= log(1.003), long if <= -log(1.003); otherwise flat. Require the entire window, no mark-price numerator and no funding sign filter. | Flatten at hourly review if premium magnitude < log(1.001), or after round(720s) minutes. Opposite entry requires exit and one-hour flat cooldown. Feature and maximum-hold durations both scale. | Segmentation/premium convergence proxy. A one-leg trade carries market beta and cannot be described as arbitrage or a hedged basis return. |

| `taker_flow_drift` | Hourly at :01, compute (2 × sum(taker-buy volume) − sum(total volume)) / sum(total volume) over prior round(60s) completed native minutes with one extra publication minute. Long if >=0.05; short if <=−0.05. No price confirmation. | Flatten on hourly review when magnitude <0.02 or sign opposes the held direction, or after round(360s) minutes. Exit then fixed one-hour flat cooldown before any reentry. Only window/maximum-hold durations scale. | Aggregate aggressive-flow continuation conjecture; not L2 OFI, execution queue advantage or demonstrated crypto alpha. |

For the three hysteresis candidates, a position retains its entry direction between reviews. On a sign reversal, flatten first, then begin the fixed one-hour cooldown; do not instantly reverse. Exit reviews are not blocked by the entry cooldown. Funding/crowding and premium windows do not use OHLC volume to infer liquidation prints, order flow or open interest: those quantities are unavailable. The fifth hypothesis additionally uses authentic aggregate taker-buy volume; it does not reveal individual order histories or queue state. Total volume still constrains execution through the established participation approximation.

## Fixed experiment and accounting

Use the audited Engine without package changes, eight separate $12,500 cash sleeves, native linear perpetuals, isolated leverage one, and fixed entry quantity equal to 25% of current sleeve equity divided by the observed native price, rounded down to the instrument lot. Quantity changes only on direction transition, not each minute. A prior partially filled/cancelled order must be resolved causally; delayed exit orders must not be cancelled and reissued on every unchanged signal. Portfolio netting, partial fills, trading fees, actual funding and mark liquidation remain Engine behavior.

Train only 2025-10-05 through 2026-04-05. Choose one common scale per candidate on pooled training daily Sharpe; ties prefer 1.0, then larger scale. Undefined/empty Sharpe must not be converted into favorable evidence: retain an explicit zero-activity/undefined-score record and choose the deterministic default only if all scores are undefined. Validation comprises fresh-wallet April5–May5, May5–June5 and June5–July5 folds. Reused evaluation is July5–October5. Earlier available observations supply causal warmup; no later values do. Retain all three training/evaluation scales and the chosen stress trials, including negative results: five candidates × eight assets × ten cases = 400 Engine simulations.

Base taker fee is 5 bps; slippage is 2 bps BTC/ETH, 8 bps DOGE/PEPE, 5 bps others; participation 5%. Joint stress doubles fees/slippage, delays one minute and halves participation. Record minute/funding-clock equity during evaluation and approximately hourly training/validation. Aggregate union timestamps with past-only per-sleeve carry-forward; never future-fill or sum only intersecting funding clocks.

Keep the same preregistered historical hurdles as the directional scalar cohort: positive compounded validation, chosen evaluation and joint stress; corresponding indicative exit-reserve returns positive; at least two of three anchored evaluation months positive; evaluation Sharpe >= 0.5; minute drawdown <=20%; all three neighboring evaluation returns positive with reserve; at least five of eight profitable evaluation sleeves; zero actual liquidation events across validation, all evaluation scales and stress. This cohort contains no deliberately hedged long/short basket, so no basket-breadth exception applies. Open positions remain genuinely marked and disclosed at the endpoint. Indicative adverse-slippage/exit-fee reserves are analysis diagnostics, not fabricated trades, cash flows or volume guarantees.

CPI event counts, active minutes, traded symbols and monthly exposures must be reported. A sparse event candidate can fail standard monthly/breadth hurdles and must remain a failure; the hurdles must not be relaxed after seeing scarcity or returns. Even a nominal pass with very few macro releases is weak evidence: eight simultaneous exposures to one CPI release are not eight independent announcements.

Cumulative declared comparison count becomes 24 only if all five candidates are adopted and run. Conditional familywise bootstrap uses fixed .05/(2×24) and 1-.05/(2×24) quantiles, with an explicit reused-data/adaptive-research limitation. Three duration scales are retained trials, not three independent economic families. Report raw family/candidate counts and all parameter trials, including prior nineteen unchanged. Conditional bootstrap adjustment cannot remove research adaptation, survivorship or dependence bias.

## Required blind causality tests before numerical outcomes

1. Build synthetic QQQ sessions spanning US DST, an early close, a weekend and holiday. A session close must not change any signal before next UTC day availability. Future closes and any future adjusted-price column may be arbitrarily perturbed without affecting earlier decisions. Session ordering and missing-session behavior must be deterministic.
2. CPI event fixtures must distinguish reference month from release time, verify ET daylight/standard conversion, retain exact first-release decimal values, reject nonconsecutive reference-month acceleration, and show no position at embargo time. Later revisions, future events and fake consensus columns cannot alter earlier signals. Do not forward-fill event direction beyond the fixed holding deadline.
3. Perturb the next actual funding settlement rate and all later rates while preserving earlier history: positions before their +one-minute availability must be unchanged. At actual settlement, cash may change for an existing position while its predictor remains unchanged until publication lag expires. Test millisecond timestamp jitter.
4. Prefix/suffix perturbation of every spot/perp source must leave earlier premium signals unchanged. Native 1000PEPE price normalization belongs only to features; quantities and settlement cash retain native units. Multiply both matched feature-price series by a constant and show identical premium signals. Reject asynchronous feeds; never use a mark/spot premium as a trade/spot premium.
5. For every hypothesis, synthetic long and short cases verify warmup, fixed entry quantity, phase cutoffs, risk exits, 0/1-bar execution delay, partial fills, pending-exit retention, stale data and available_at boundaries. Adversarial high/low bars must not imply executable prices at signal time. Exits use actual subsequent Engine fills.
6. Test grouped pooled equity with nonmatching funding clocks, actual endpoint settlement, marked open inventory, fee/funding conservation, and ms/us/ns timestamp endpoint postprocessing. Reserve subtraction must leave saved execution ledgers unchanged.
7. Flow synthetic fixtures verify exact volume-weighted imbalance rather than averaging minute ratios, both signs, bounds, zero aggregate volume, native bundle-unit invariance, gap reset and extra publication minute. Perturb all unpublished buy/total volumes and require earlier signals unchanged. A price change without changing volume inputs must not alter this factor.
8. Prove common-scale selection reads training rows only; perturb validation/evaluation metrics in a fixture and require identical chosen scales. Freeze source hashes, the external raw records, transformed availability tables, protocol, model defaults and every parameter trial before any market-outcome inspection.

This document is a proposal, not an executed protocol or numerical strategy recommendation. Implementation or schema details requiring change must be resolved and frozen before running this cohort; no current nineteen-policy source or result is modified by it.
